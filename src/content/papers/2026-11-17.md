### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper studies how to form and retrieve long-term memory for intelligent agents from heterogeneous digital resources (webpages, video transcripts, GitHub repos, papers, notes). The authors process a 100-resource collection (96 usable) and evaluate five memory/retrieval approaches: BM25, dense RAG, summary-based memory, structured-fact memory, and graph-linked memory. Using a 150-question set (120 test) they measure retrieval effectiveness (Recall@5, MRR, nDCG@5), answer accuracy, evidence support, fact-extraction quality, provenance preservation, and storage/retrieval efficiency. Graph-linked memory attains the best retrieval metrics and highest answer accuracy, while structured-fact memory shows superior fact-extraction F1 and provenance with lower storage and latency, illustrating trade-offs among representations.

**中文**

本文研究如何从异构数字资源（网页、视频转录、GitHub 仓库、研究论文、个人笔记）为智能体形成并检索长期记忆。作者处理了100个资源（96个可用），比较了五种记忆/检索方法：BM25、密集检索增强生成、基于摘要的记忆、结构化事实记忆和图链接记忆。使用150道问题集（120道测试），评估指标包含检索效果（Recall@5、MRR、nDCG@5）、答案准确率、证据支持、事实抽取质量、来源保存以及存储/检索效率。结果显示图链接记忆在检索指标和答案准确率上最好，而结构化事实记忆在事实抽取F1和来源得分上更优且存储与延迟更低，表明了表示形式之间的权衡。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper studies a lightweight graph-guided two-hop retrieval method for knowledge-intensive question answering over scientific-publication metadata. The authors build a small typed-node graph (authors, papers, venues, years) and use an author anchor plus topical similarity to restrict and rank candidate papers; a second graph hop returns the requested property together with its evidence path. The evaluation, constructed deterministically from 43 checked ACL Anthology records, produces 688 two-hop metadata questions. The graph approach achieves 99.42% exact-match and correct-path recovery, substantially outperforming TF-IDF/BM25 retrieval over fragmented atomic facts, while denormalized full-record baselines can match graph performance. The study runs without neural training on CPU.

**中文**

本文提出一种面向科学出版物元数据的轻量级图引导两跳检索方法，用于知识密集型问答。作者构建了一个带类型的节点图（作者、论文、会议、年份），以作者锚点和主题相似性来限制并排序候选论文，第二跳返回目标属性及其证据路径。评估基于 43 条经人工核验的 ACL Anthology 记录，确定性生成 688 个两跳元数据问题。图方法在准确匹配与正确路径恢复上达到 99.42%，显著优于在碎片化原子事实上的 TF-IDF/BM25，但去归一化的完整记录基线可以达到相近表现。该实验在不做神经训练的 CPU 环境下完成。

**中文**
