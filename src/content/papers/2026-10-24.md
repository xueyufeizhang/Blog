### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper studies hybrid retrieval for early-stage furniture carbon screening, where designers need computable, traceable answers tied to governing standards. The authors combine a knowledge graph capable of executing carbon calculations with a GraphRAG text index that recovers conditional standards guidance. They evaluate these components on 100 furniture-carbon questions spanning five primary conditions, using oracle and routing analyses to ask whether complementarity between sources implies query-time selectability. Results show retrieval value is task-dependent: large gains for factual lookups (improvement 0.5600) but negligible gains for standards interpretation; concurrent fusion offered little additional recoverable headroom. Outputs are material-stage screening estimates derived from author-compiled bills of materials.

**中文**

该论文研究用于家具碳排放早期筛查的混合检索，面向需要可计算且可追溯地对应标准的设计场景。作者将能执行碳计算的知识图谱（KG）与恢复条件性标准指引的 GraphRAG 文本索引结合。基于 100 个跨五类情形的问题，采用 oracle 与路由分析评估互补性是否能转化为查询时的可选性。结果表明检索价值与任务类别相关：对事实查找收益显著（提升 0.5600），但对标准解释几乎无收益；并行融合未能回收大量可用的上限空间。输出为基于作者汇编物料清单的物料阶段估算。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both this paper and the thesis study hybrid use of structured knowledge graphs and text retrieval and analyze when structured access improves task accuracy. Differences/limitations: the paper is domain-specific (furniture carbon screening), uses a small (100-question) benchmark, and focuses on source selectability and oracle/routing diagnostics rather than persistent, incrementally constructed graph memory, multi-hop retrieval, agentic RAG, or entity-resolution strategies. Complementarity: the paper's routing analyses, oracle diagnostics, and the emphasis on inspectable clause context could inform query-aware graph memory, source-selection heuristics, and benchmark design in the thesis.

**中文**

重合点：该论文与本论文都研究结构化知识图谱（KG）与文本检索的混合使用，并分析何时结构化访问能提升任务准确性。差异/局限：该研究具有领域特定性（家具碳筛查）、样本量较小（100 问题），侧重于源选择和 oracle/路由诊断，而不是持久化、增量构建的图记忆、多跳检索、智能体式 RAG（检索增强生成（RAG））或实体解析策略。互补性：其路由分析、oracle 诊断方法以及对可审查条款上下文的强调，可为查询感知图记忆、源选择启发式和基准设计提供参考。
