### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper presents WCVP-KG, an RDF representation of the World Checklist of Vascular Plants (WCVP). The authors convert the expert-curated WCVP (≈1.45M plant names as of Aug 2026) into a dereferenceable knowledge graph using Darwin Core RDF, modeling nomenclatural information and taxonomic concepts primarily with SKOS and complementing with OWL classes where appropriate. The KG preserves rich taxonomic statuses and metadata, is published under CC-BY-4.0 with a DOI, and is designed for FAIR interoperability. The authors argue that WCVP-KG can serve as a semantic backbone to enable graph-based ML, link prediction, and GraphRAG-style multi-hop reasoning across biodiversity resources.

**中文**

本文将世界维管植物名录（WCVP）表示为 RDF 格式的知识图谱（WCVP-KG）。作者将专家审核的 WCVP（截至 2026 年 8 月约 1.45M 植物名）转换为可解析的知识图谱，基于 Darwin Core RDF 建模，其中将命名学信息和分类学概念主要用 SKOS 表示，并在适当处辅以 OWL 类。该图谱保留了丰富的分类状态与元数据，以 CC-BY-4.0 发布并有 DOI，旨在实现 FAIR 互操作性。作者还指出 WCVP-KG 可作为语义骨干，支持图表示学习、连边预测以及基于 GraphRAG 的跨数据集多跳推理。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both work with knowledge graphs to support multi-hop retrieval and argue for GraphRAG-style reasoning and verifiable grounding. Differences: WCVP-KG is a static, expert-curated domain KG converted from a Darwin Core archive, not an incrementally assembled graph memory produced by agent interactions; the paper does not address entity-resolution trade-offs, agentic paradigms, local LLM orchestration, or empirical RAG/agent evaluations described in the thesis. Complementarity: WCVP-KG could serve as an authoritative external KG to link against the thesis's agent-constructed graph memory and as a domain benchmark for evaluating entity linking, multi-hop traversal, and verifiability.

**中文**

重合点：两者均使用知识图谱支持多跳检索，并提出利用 GraphRAG 式的推理与可验证的知识支撑。差异：WCVP-KG 是从 Darwin Core 存档转换而来的静态、专家维护领域 KG，而非由智能体交互增量构建的图记忆；论文未讨论实体消歧的折中、智能体范式、本地 LLM 编排或论文中提出的 RAG/智能体实证评估。互补性：WCVP-KG 可作为权威的外部 KG，用于与论文所构建的图记忆建立映射，并可作为评估实体链接、多跳路径遍历与可验证性的领域基准。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this work as a concrete example of producing a FAIR, dereferenceable domain KG from a widely used biodiversity source; highlight its SKOS-based distinction between names and taxa as a modeling pattern. When reviewing related work, note the paper's emphasis on interoperability (Darwin Core RDF) and machine-readable IRIs rather than agentic memory or incremental assembly.

**中文**

可将此工作作为从广泛使用的生物多样性资源生成 FAIR、可解析领域 KG 的具体示例；强调其以 SKOS 区分“名称”与“分类单元”的建模模式。在相关工作综述中应指出论文更侧重互操作性（Darwin Core RDF）与机器可解析 IRI，而非智能体记忆或增量组装。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Use WCVP-KG as an external reference for entity linking experiments: evaluate how the thesis's entity-resolution strategies (embedding-similarity vs LLM-as-judge) map extracted surface forms to WCVP IRIs and handle synonyms/orthographic variants. Treat WCVP taxonomic statuses and SKOS synonym relations as typed edges or confidence signals when integrating into agentic graph memory, and measure effects on multi-hop path completeness and verifiability.

**中文**

可将 WCVP-KG 用作外部参考进行实体链接实验：评估论文中提出的实体消歧策略（嵌入相似度 vs LLM 评判者）如何将抽取到的表面形式映射到 WCVP IRI，并处理同义词/拼写变体。将 WCVP 的分类状态与 SKOS 同义关系视为集成到智能体图记忆时的带类型边或置信度信号，度量其对多跳路径完整性与可验证性的影响。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate WCVP-KG with an agentic system that incrementally assembles graph memory and evaluates online entity linking to WCVP IRIs; investigate how linking to a curated taxonomic KG affects fragmentation, multi-hop retrieval accuracy, and latency. Explore automated mapping between extracted triples and the WCVP SKOS/OWL structures to support hybrid query-aware graph memory.

**中文**

将 WCVP-KG 与增量组装图记忆的智能体系统集成，并评估在线实体链接到 WCVP IRI 的效果；研究连接到经过策划的分类学 KG 如何影响图碎片化、多跳检索准确率与延迟。探索将抽取的三元组自动映射到 WCVP 的 SKOS/OWL 结构，以支持混合的查询感知图记忆。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper proposes the Intent-Driven Cognitive Architecture (IDCA), a reference architecture for LLM-native operating environments that elevates Semantic Processing Units (SPUs), Vector-Graph Memory Units (VGMUs), Omnimodal Sensory I/O, Intent Convergence Loops, and AI-native IPC to first-class primitives. Framing Von Neumann hardware as the execution substrate, the authors present a semi-formal execution model, typed interfaces and runtime invariants, and an evaluation agenda centered on four falsifiable hypotheses. The paper is primarily conceptual but includes a reference prototype used to exercise the proposed hypotheses, including verifier-gated convergence and relational memory retrieval via GraphRAG, alongside ablation-style analyses.

**中文**

本文提出了意图驱动认知架构（IDCA），作为面向LLM的操作环境参考架构，将语义处理单元（SPU）、向量-图记忆单元（VGMU）、全模态感知I/O、意图收敛回路和AI原生进程间通信设定为一阶原语。作者在将冯·诺依曼硬件视为执行基底的前提下，给出半形式化执行模型、类型化接口与运行时不变量，并提出以四个可证伪假设为中心的评估议程。论文以概念性贡献为主，并附带一个参考原型，用以验证诸如验证器门控收敛和通过GraphRAG进行关系性记忆检索的假设，同时包含消融分析。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: IDCA explicitly includes Vector-Graph Memory Units and references relational memory retrieval via GraphRAG, directly relevant to the thesis's graph memory and agentic RAG aims. Differences: the paper is chiefly a high-level architectural and conceptual contribution with a prototype, whereas the thesis is an empirical investigation of incremental KG-style graph memory, entity resolution strategies, and multi-hop QA benchmarks. Complementarity: IDCA's typed interfaces, execution model, and verifier-gated convergence concept could provide useful design patterns and evaluation hypotheses for implementing and evaluating the thesis's system.

**中文**

重合点：IDCA 明确包含向量-图记忆单元，且提及通过 GraphRAG 的关系性记忆检索，与本论文关于图记忆和智能体式 RAG 的目标直接相关。差异：该论文主要是高层次的架构性与概念性贡献并带有原型，而本硕士论文侧重于增量构建的类知识图谱图记忆、实体解析策略以及多跳问答的实证评估。互补性：IDCA 的类型化接口、执行模型以及验证器门控收敛概念可为论文的系统实现与评估提供可借鉴的设计模式和假设。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this work when framing system-level design choices or arguing for standard interfaces between memory, reasoning, and tool modules. Be cautious: the paper is conceptual—avoid treating its prototype demonstrations as comprehensive empirical validation of GraphRAG-style graph memory in multi-hop QA.

**中文**

在论述系统级设计选择或主张记忆、推理与工具模块之间的标准接口时可引用此工作。但需谨慎：该论文以概念性为主——不要将其原型演示视为对 GraphRAG 式图记忆在多跳问答中充分的实证验证。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt IDCA's notion of typed interfaces to specify a clear API for the thesis's graph memory (VGMU) and LLM agents, enabling modular ablations. Consider reusing the paper's evaluation agenda and falsifiable-hypothesis framing to structure experiments (e.g., verifier-gated convergence for verifiability). Map verifier-gated stopping or convergence tests onto the thesis's bridge-entity and verifiability metrics, but do not assume the paper's prototype results generalize to your local-model setup.

**中文**

采用 IDCA 的类型化接口概念，为论文中的图记忆（VGMU）与 LLM 智能体指定明确的 API，从而便于模块化消融实验。考虑借用论文的评估议程和可证伪假设框架来组织实验（如用于可验证性的验证器门控收敛）。将验证器门控的停止或收敛测试映射到论文的桥接实体和可验证性指标上，但不要假定该论文原型的结论可以直接推广到你在本地模型上的设置。

### Future directions

**English**

Explore concretizing IDCA primitives by implementing a VGMU-aligned graph memory with the thesis's triple extraction and entity-resolution pipelines, and evaluate verifier-gated convergence mechanisms on MultiHop-RAG. Another direction is to formalize the runtime invariants for graph coherence and measure their effect on multi-hop retrieval accuracy and latency.

**中文**

可进一步将 IDCA 原语具体化：实现一个与 VGMU 对齐的图记忆模块，结合论文的三元组抽取与实体解析流水线，并在 MultiHop-RAG 上评估验证器门控收敛机制。另一个方向是对图连贯性的运行时不变量进行形式化，并测量其对多跳检索准确性与延迟的影响。

**English**

The paper reports a controlled, reproducible benchmark, explicit negative findings about GraphRAG, and an audit of failure modes; these elements are directly relevant to evaluating structured graph memory claims and experimental design in the thesis.

**中文**

该论文提供了可复现的受控基准、关于 GraphRAG 的负面发现以及对失败模式的审计；这些要点对评估结构化图记忆主张和实验设计与本论文高度相关。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper presents a reproducible benchmark in Mexican labor law to evaluate outcome prediction and statutory grounding separately. Ninety attorney-authored scenarios with gold outcomes and statutory citations and a 330-document retrieval corpus are used to compare four architectures—parametric-only, RAG, GraphRAG, and reward-guided best-of-N—across five quantized 7B–9B backbones (20 architecture–backbone conditions). Dense retrieval improved statutory grounding but did not improve outcome prediction; GraphRAG showed no measurable advantage over standard RAG under the evaluated implementation. The authors also report significant architecture-by-backbone variability and an audit that identified multiple measurement and configuration failure modes.

**中文**

本文构建了一个可复现的墨西哥劳动法基准，分别评估裁决预测与法条依据能力。基准包含90个由律师撰写并带有金标准结果和法条引用的场景，以及由330份文档组成的检索语料库。作者在五种量化的7B–9B骨干模型上比较了参数生成、检索增强生成（RAG）、GraphRAG 和基于奖励的 best-of-N 四种架构（共20种条件）。密集检索提升了法条依据，但未改善结果预测；在所评估的实现下，GraphRAG 对比标准 RAG 无明显优势。研究还报告了架构与骨干模型的交互差异，并通过审计发现多种测量与配置失误。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both work on comparing structured graph augmentation (GraphRAG) with RAG-style retrieval and measure whether graph augmentation improves downstream tasks. Differences: this paper studies a legal decision-support task with static, domain-specific documents and does not report using agentic behavior or a persistently accumulated, query-aware graph memory built incrementally from agent interactions. Limitations: negative results here caution that GraphRAG may not help outcome prediction in some domains or implementations. Complementarity: the paper’s controlled benchmark, audit of failure modes, and backbone sensitivity analyses are directly useful when evaluating whether structured long-term graph memory improves multi-hop retrieval and QA in the thesis’s agentic setting.

**中文**

重合点：两者都比较了结构化图增强（GraphRAG）与 RAG 检索，并评估图增强是否能提升下游任务。差异：该论文在法律决策支持的静态领域语料上进行实验，未报告使用智能体行为或从交互中增量构建的、具有查询感知的持久图记忆。局限性：其负结果提示在某些领域或特定实现下，GraphRAG 未必能改善结果预测。互补性：论文提供的受控基准、对失败模式的审计以及对骨干模型敏感性的分析，对本论文在评估结构化长期图记忆是否改进多跳检索与问答时具有直接参考价值。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Report the paper’s negative findings and its audit prominently when positioning related work: they underline implementation sensitivity and the need to separate grounding from task accuracy. Cite its backbone-by-architecture interaction as evidence that averaged comparisons can obscure important variability.

**中文**

在相关工作中突出该文的负面结果及其审计结论：这些结果强调实现细节的敏感性以及将法条依据与任务准确率分开的必要性。引用其关于架构与骨干模型交互的发现，说明平均化比较可能掩盖重要差异。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt the paper’s separated measurements for grounding versus outcome accuracy and its controlled architecture-by-backbone design to expose interactions. Emulate their audit practice to detect measurement/configuration failure modes. For direct comparison with the thesis, apply GraphRAG, RAG, and agentic RAG variants on the same benchmark while instrumenting whether graph augmentation is persistent, incremental, and query-aware and logging per-query graph usage, bridge-entity coverage, and latency.

**中文**

采纳该文将法条依据与结果准确率分离的测量方法，以及受控的架构×骨干设计以揭示交互效应。借鉴其审计实践以发现测量/配置失误。为与本论文直接比较，应在同一基准上同时运行 GraphRAG、RAG 与带图记忆的智能体 RAG 变体，并记录图增强是否为持久、增量且具查询感知，以及逐查询的图使用情况、桥接实体覆盖率和延迟。

### Future directions

**English**

Evaluate GraphRAG-style augmentation within an agentic system that incrementally constructs and reuses a persistent, query-aware graph memory, explicitly measuring entity-resolution quality and multi-hop path reuse. Investigate backbone sensitivity under local, quantized models and report verifiability/grounding metrics separately from task accuracy.

**中文**

在能够增量构建并重用持久、查询感知图记忆的智能体系统中评估 GraphRAG 式增强，明确测量实体解析质量与多跳路径的重用情况。研究在本地量化模型下的骨干敏感性，并将可验证性/法条依据指标与任务准确率分开报告。
