### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper introduces GRADE, a framework for query-adaptive multi-hop reasoning over knowledge graphs. GRADE integrates score-gap adaptive pruning, a four-dimensional path scoring scheme, evidence-guided gated propagation, and topology-aware marginal-relevance reranking; it maps mean path quality scores to a bounded support signal via a fixed binary Dirichlet mapping for state propagation. Empirical results reported with GPT-4o show strong answer and path F1 on WebQSP and CWQ, and high reference answer hit rates and exact path matches on a KG4DT chemical process subgraph. The contribution is a set of algorithms for candidate-path selection, scoring, and traceable path selection on existing KGs.

本文提出 GRADE，一个用于查询自适应的知识图谱（KG）多跳推理框架。GRADE 结合了得分差自适应剪枝、四维路径评分、证据引导的门控传播和拓扑感知的边际相关性重排；通过固定二元 Dirichlet 映射将平均路径质量得分转换为有界的支持信号以用于状态传播。文中在 WebQSP、CWQ（使用 GPT-4o）以及 KG4DT 化工子图上的实验证明了较高的答案与路径 F1、参考答案命中率以及精确路径匹配率。该工作主要贡献于对已有 KG 上候选路径选择、评分与可追溯路径选择的算法设计。

**English**

Complementarity: GRADE’s adaptive pruning, path scoring, and topology-aware reranking methods could be adapted as path-ranking and confidence-propagation modules within the thesis’s evolving graph memory, but GRADE does not address incremental construction, entity resolution, or local-agent latency trade-offs.

**中文**
