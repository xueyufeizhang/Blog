**English**

Proposes a novel differentiable bridge to inject structural graph information into LLM prompts and a semantic-guided distillation to reduce annotation need—techniques that could materially inform retrieval scoring and prompt design in graph memory systems. Although supervised and training-heavy, the methodological ideas are potentially transferable to agentic, incremental-memory settings.

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses limitations of decoupled GraphRAG pipelines—objective misalignment between retrieval and generation and dependence on costly annotations—by proposing UniGFM-RAG, an end-to-end framework that tightly couples a Graph Foundation Model (GFM) retriever with an LLM generator. The GFM retrieves and scores relevant graph nodes; a fully differentiable graph bridge projects node embeddings into soft-weighted graph prefixes that are combined with raw document text to form graph-augmented prompts. Answer loss is back-propagated through the bridge to jointly optimize retrieval and generation, and a semantic-guided distillation module supplies auxiliary pseudo-supervision from query-document semantic similarities. The authors report consistent gains over strong baselines on several multi-hop QA and out-of-domain GraphRAG benchmarks.

**中文**

本文针对传统解耦的 GraphRAG 流水线存在的检索与生成目标错位及对昂贵标注的依赖，提出 UniGFM-RAG——一个将图基础模型（GFM）检索器与大语言模型生成器紧密耦合的端到端框架。GFM 对图节点进行推理与打分；一个全可微的图桥将节点嵌入投影为软加权的图前缀，并与原始文档文本结合形成图增强提示。最终答案损失可以反向传播穿过图桥以联合优化检索与生成，同时引入语义引导的蒸馏模块，利用查询-文档语义相似性生成伪标签作为辅助监督。作者在若干多跳问答和域外 GraphRAG 基准上报告了相较强基线的一致性提升。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both work on GraphRAG-style multi-hop retrieval and reasoning and aim to improve retrieval-reasoning alignment. Differences: UniGFM-RAG targets supervised, end-to-end training with a differentiable graph bridge and semantic distillation, while the thesis studies an online, incrementally assembled persistent graph memory constructed from agent interactions and focuses on entity resolution, agentic RAG paradigms, and local/inference latency constraints. Limitations relative to the thesis: the paper does not address incremental, runtime-assembled graph memory, entity resolution strategies for surface-form variation, or deployment with constrained local models and agentic workflows. Complementarity: the paper's differentiable bridge and pseudo-label distillation could inform retrieval scoring or soft-prefix prompt techniques in an agentic, incremental graph-memory setting.

**中文**

重合点：两者都针对 GraphRAG 式的多跳检索与推理，关注检索与推理的协调。差异：UniGFM-RAG 面向监督的端到端训练，使用可微图桥与语义蒸馏；而本论文聚焦于由智能体交互增量构建的持久图记忆、实体解析、不同智能体范式以及本地化/延时约束。相对于本论文的局限：该候选工作未讨论运行时增量构建的图记忆、处理实体表面形式差异的实体解析方法，亦未针对受限本地模型或智能体式工作流进行部署评估。互补性：其可微图桥与伪标签蒸馏方法可为在智能体、增量图记忆环境中改进检索打分或软前缀提示技术提供参考。

Adapt semantic-guided distillation to generate pseudo-relevance labels for graph edges or bridge-entity linking when gold annotations are unavailable. Evaluate any added training-time benefits against the thesis' low-latency, incremental graph-memory constraints and measure trade-offs in end-to-end latency.

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper presents TenderMatch, a graph retrieval-augmented generation (GraphRAG) framework for procurement recommendation that enforces retrieval of permitted evidence before recommending lots. Two controller styles are compared: a fixed controller that follows a prescribed retrieval schedule and an adaptive controller that may select one additional tool or stop after mandatory retrieval. Experiments use TenderKG (6,126 procedures, 29,220 lots, 15,340 companies) and a grouped retrospective holdout. Matched validation favors adaptive retrieval, but a frozen 20-company test shows no consistent advantage (Recall@10: adaptive 0.20435 vs fixed 0.20217; nDCG@10: adaptive 0.10722 vs fixed 0.11212). BM25 with E5 reranking attains the highest test nDCG@10 (0.11379). The contribution emphasizes an explicit evidence boundary and a controlled comparison of retrieval policies, noting structural checks do not guarantee supplier eligibility or semantic sufficiency.

**中文**

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses scientific reasoning over large domain-specific knowledge graphs that cannot be fully provided to LLMs. It trains a compact model, Prospector, to traverse a domain KG step-by-step, calling a small set of tools to incrementally build context and produce an evidence chain verifiable edge by edge against retrieved graph content. Evaluated on a semiconductor-manufacturing KG, Prospector approaches strong off-the-shelf models given the same graph, transfers to unseen graphs in other fields, runs on a single GPU with low cost and latency, and keeps proprietary data local. The abstract highlights transferability, latent-state interpretability, and controllable behavior modes.

**中文**

论文针对无法完整提供给 LLM 的大型领域知识图谱（KG）上的科学推理问题，训练了一个紧凑模型 Prospector 来逐步遍历图谱。Prospector 使用一小组工具逐步构建上下文，并生成可逐边核验的证据链，与从图中检索到的内容逐条对照。作者在半导体制造的知识图谱上评估，结果接近在相同图与工具下的强基线模型，并能迁移到生物仿生材料、PFAS 与材料性质等未见图谱。模型可在单 GPU 上运行，具有较低成本和延迟，并保持专有数据在本地。摘要强调了迁移性、潜在态的可解释性与行为调控能力。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works use knowledge graphs to ground LLM reasoning, emphasize graph traversal and producing verifiable evidence chains, and target private/local deployment and efficiency. Differences: Prospector is trained to traverse a pre-built domain KG, whereas the thesis focuses on incrementally assembled graph memory created from the system's own triple extraction and agentic RAG interactions. Limitations relative to the thesis: the abstract does not address entity resolution, persistent long-term graph construction from agent activity, or comparisons among agentic paradigms. Complementarity: Prospector's compact traversal policy and transfer results could be integrated as a traversal controller within the thesis's evolving graph memory to improve multi-hop path selection and latency.

**中文**

重合点：两者都使用知识图谱（KG）为 LLM 推理提供支撑，强调图遍历与可核验的证据链，并关注本地/私有部署与效率。差异：Prospector 被训练为在预先构建的领域 KG 上遍历，而本论文主题关注的是由系统自身的三元组抽取与智能体交互逐步构建的图记忆。与本论文相比的限制：摘要未涉及实体解析、来自智能体活动的持久长期图构建，或对不同智能体范式的比较。互补性：Prospector 的紧凑遍历策略与迁移性结果可被纳入论文提出的演化图记忆中，作为路径选择或延迟优化的控制器。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this paper when discussing learned traversal policies, verifiable chain-of-evidence outputs, and private/local deployment of KG-based reasoning. Emphasize the distinction between traversal policies trained on a static KG and systems that assemble structured memory online from agent interactions.

**中文**

在讨论学习型遍历策略、可核验的证据链输出以及基于 KG 的私有/本地推理时应引用此文。强调在静态图上训练的遍历策略与从智能体交互在线组装结构化记忆的系统之间的区别。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Experimentally compare a learned traversal policy (Prospector-style) against agentic planners that operate over an incrementally assembled graph memory: measure multi-hop retrieval accuracy, bridge-entity reuse, and end-to-end latency. Use edge-by-edge verification metrics for evidence chains and evaluate transfer of the traversal policy when graph topology changes as memory accumulates. Record latent-policy states to analyze and potentially intervene in behavior modes.

**中文**

在方法上将学习型遍历策略（类似 Prospector）与在渐进组装的图记忆上运行的智能体规划器进行对比实验：测量多跳检索准确率、桥接实体复用率和端到端延迟。采用逐边核验的证据链度量，并评估当图记忆累积导致图拓扑变化时遍历策略的迁移能力。记录潜在策略状态以分析并可能干预其行为模式。

### Future directions

**English**

Train or fine-tune traversal policies directly on incrementally constructed graph memories produced by agentic RAG, and jointly learn entity-resolution modules so the policy can operate robustly under aliasing. Evaluate combinations of Prospector-style policies with different agentic paradigms (ReAct, Plan-and-Execute, Self-Ask, Reflexion) on MultiHop-RAG-like benchmarks.

**中文**

将遍历策略直接在由智能体 RAG 逐步构建的图记忆上训练或微调，并联合学习实体解析模块，使策略在别名存在时也能稳健运行。评估 Prospector 式策略与不同智能体范式（ReAct、Plan-and-Execute、Self-Ask、Reflexion）的组合，在 MultiHop-RAG 类基准上进行比较。
