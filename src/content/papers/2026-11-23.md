### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper studies consistency failures in asynchronous agent-memory services where writes are acknowledged quickly but background workers extract and commit facts later. It formalizes two failure modes—read-after-write staleness (readers seeing superseded facts before a commit) and history erasure (in-place updates that remove earlier values)—and measures them under deterministic lag and commit-reordering models using the LongMemEval-S benchmark. The authors propose DeltaWAL, which writes raw turns to a write-ahead log with monotonic epochs and merges uncommitted log tails gated by relevance; DeltaWAL-Versioned additionally retains every fact version. Reported results show large gains in knowledge-update question accuracy (11.7% → 96.7% with glm-5.3) and ablations attribute most benefit to the relevance gate; keeping versions prevents history erasure. The paper also audits ordering behavior on MemConflict and reproduces erasure in an OSS store, offering practical system-level remedies.

**中文**

本文研究了异步智能体记忆服务中的一致性问题：写入先被确认但后台工作进程延后提取并提交事实。作者形式化了两类失败——写后读陈旧性（在提交前读取到过时事实）和历史抹除（就地更新导致早期值丢失）——并在确定性延迟与提交重排序模型下，使用 LongMemEval-S 进行测量。作者提出 DeltaWAL：将原始对话写入带单调 epoch 的写前日志（write-ahead log），并对未提交的日志尾部进行相关性门控合并；DeltaWAL-Versioned 则保留每个事实的全部版本。论文报告在知识更新问题上的显著提升（用 glm-5.3 从 11.7% 提升到 96.7%），消融实验表明相关性门控是主要贡献；保留版本能防止历史抹除。同时在 MemConflict 上审计了顺序行为，并在一个开源存储中复现了抹除问题，给出了可行的系统级修复。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both work on persistent long-term memory for LLM-driven agents and on verifiability and correctness of stored facts. Differences: this paper focuses on storage consistency, versioning, and asynchronous pipelines rather than on structured graph memory, triple extraction, entity resolution, or multi-hop graph traversal. Complementarity: the DeltaWAL and versioning techniques are directly relevant as a durable backing-store design for a thesis graph memory and its evaluation; their staleness and history-erasure tests could be reused to validate that the evolving knowledge graph preserves past triples and answers temporal queries.

**中文**

重合点：两者都关注为 LLM 驱动的智能体提供持久化记忆，以及已存事实的可验证性与正确性。差异：该论文侧重于存储一致性、版本化和异步流水线，而非结构化的图记忆、三元组抽取、实体解析或多跳图遍历。互补性：DeltaWAL 与版本化方案可作为论文中图记忆的持久化后端设计，其对写后读陈旧性和历史抹除的测试可用于验证不断演进的知识图谱是否保留了先前三元组并能回答时序查询。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adopting DeltaWAL's write-ahead-log with monotonic epochs as the ingestion layer for the graph store, merging uncommitted turns with stored triples by relevance rather than simple newer-wins. Reproduce their deterministic lag and commit-reordering evaluations (LongMemEval-S style) to measure read-after-write staleness for triple-level writes, and include a versioned-store ablation to detect history erasure of triples and reasoning paths. Measure both accuracy on knowledge-update queries and end-to-end latency to assess trade-offs.

**中文**

考虑将 DeltaWAL 的带单调 epoch 的写前日志作为图存储的摄取层，通过相关性而非简单的 newer-wins 策略将未提交对话与已存三元组合并。复现其确定性延迟与提交重排序评估（类似 LongMemEval-S）以度量三元组写入的写后读陈旧性，并加入版本化存储消融以检测三元组与推理路径的历史抹除。对知识更新查询的准确性和端到端延迟同时进行度量以评估折衷。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate DeltaWAL-style versioning with the thesis's graph memory so that every extracted triple and reasoning step is versioned and queryable; extend relevance gating to be query-aware so merges prefer context-relevant paths. Also study how version retention interacts with entity-resolution strategies and multi-hop retrieval accuracy.

**中文**

将 DeltaWAL 式的版本化与论文的图记忆集成，使每个抽取的三元组和推理步骤都被版本化并可查询；将相关性门控扩展为查询感知，以便合并时偏好与上下文相关的路径。同时研究版本保留如何影响实体解析策略与多跳检索的准确性。
