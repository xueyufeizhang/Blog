### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper frames an "auditability dilemma" when deploying LLMs for credit risk management: fine-tuning (PEFT) improves behavioral alignment and formatting, but stores knowledge parametrically and can hinder regulatory traceability; RAG exposes explicit source evidence and adapts to fresh data, improving verifiability and hallucination resilience. The author conducts a comparative analysis of PEFT and RAG across factual accuracy, data freshness, operational cost, and mathematical explainability. To reconcile trade-offs, the paper proposes a hybrid blueprint combining a lightweight format-fine-tuned LLM with a Knowledge Graph–powered RAG (GraphRAG) pipeline to retain template adherence while providing auditable evidence paths.

**中文**

论文提出在信贷风险管理中部署大模型的“可审计性困境”：微调（PEFT）能改善行为一致性与格式，但依赖参数化记忆，降低监管可追溯性；检索增强生成（RAG）能公开证据来源并适应实时数据，提升可验证性和抗幻觉能力。作者在事实准确性、数据新鲜度、运营成本与数学可解释性等维度上比较了PEFT与RAG。为权衡利弊，论文提出一种混合方案：将轻量格式微调的模型与基于知识图谱的RAG（GraphRAG）管道结合，以在遵守内部模板的同时保留可审计的证据路径。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works advocate integrating knowledge-graph-backed RAG (GraphRAG) to improve verifiability and evidence provenance. Differences: the candidate paper is domain-focused (credit risk, regulatory auditability) and compares PEFT vs RAG, whereas the thesis centers on agentic RAG with incrementally constructed graph memory, multi-hop retrieval, and entity-resolution trade-offs. Complementarity: the paper's auditability and hybrid-architecture arguments can inform the thesis's verifiability metrics and motivate hybrid LLM\+graph approaches in agentic, local deployments.

**中文**

重合点：两者都主张引入基于知识图谱的RAG（GraphRAG）以增强可验证性和证据溯源。差异：该论文面向特定领域（信贷与监管可审计性），比较PEFT与RAG，而本硕士论文关注智能体驱动的RAG、增量构建的图记忆、多跳检索与实体消歧的权衡。互补性：该论文关于可审计性和混合架构的论点可为论文的可验证性度量提供参考，并推动在本地智能体部署中探索轻量微调模型与图记忆结合的方案。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this paper when discussing verifiability and regulatory-driven design goals; its hybrid GraphRAG proposal is a useful counterpoint to pure-graph or pure-vector retrieval approaches. Emphasize that the paper targets enterprise compliance, so its evaluation priorities (audit trails, template conformity) differ from multi-hop retrieval research.

**中文**

在讨论可验证性与监管驱动的设计目标时引用此文；其混合GraphRAG建议是对纯图检索或纯向量检索的有益补充。注意该论文以企业合规为出发点，其评估侧重点（审计链、模板一致性）与多跳检索研究不同。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adapt the paper's auditability dimensions into the thesis evaluation: add measures for evidence traceability and conformity to decision templates alongside multi-hop accuracy. Experimentally compare a lightweight PEFT model \+ graph-memory pipeline against pure agentic graph-memory and pure RAG baselines, measuring verifiability, bridge-entity reuse, and latency under data drift.

**中文**

将论文的可审计性维度纳入论文评估：在多跳准确性之外加入证据可追溯性与决策模板一致性的度量。可在实验中比较轻量PEFT模型\+图记忆流水线、纯智能体图记忆以及纯RAG基线，评估可验证性、桥接实体重用率与在数据漂移下的延迟表现。

### Future directions

**English**

Investigate a concrete hybrid that combines lightweight PEFT for format and policy constraints with persistent, query-aware graph memory to support multi-hop provenance. Evaluate such hybrids on both domain benchmarks (e.g., financial decision tasks) and MultiHop-RAG to study trade-offs between auditability and multi-hop retrieval performance.

**中文**

研究将轻量PEFT用于格式与策略约束，且与持久的、查询感知图记忆结合的具体混合方案，以支持多跳溯源。将该混合模型同时在领域基准（如金融决策任务）和MultiHop-RAG上评估，以探讨可审计性与多跳检索性能之间的权衡。

**English**

Recommended for a skim: the study is a concise, well-documented paired comparison with useful reproducibility artifacts and limited scope—relevant for extractor-choice methodology but not central to graph-memory algorithms.

**中文**

建议略读：该研究是简明且有良好文档的配对比较，提供有用的可复现工件，范围有限——对提取器选择的方法论有价值，但并非图记忆算法的核心文献。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This research note examines how a writing criterion (prompt/rule) for agent memory depends on the model used to extract memories. Building on an earlier LongMemEval result where rule C2 exceeded deployed C0 by \+24.8 points with gemini-2.5-flash-lite, the study reruns three writing criteria (C0, C2, and a prompt P) with two extractor models (gemini-2.5-flash-lite and gemini-2.5-flash) on 242 questions. All memories, answers and grades were remade under a pre-registered protocol and independently reviewed. The primary contrast—the change in C2’s advantage between extractors—was −4.1 points (95% interval −12.3 to \+4.1), an inconclusive difference. Secondary contrasts show C2 &gt; C0 with both extractors. The note publishes its run, provenance, and analyses for reproducibility.

**中文**

该研究说明笔记考察了用于生成智能体记忆的写入标准（提示/规则）在多大程度上依赖于提取模型。基于早期 LongMemEval 研究中 C2 在 gemini-2.5-flash-lite 上比部署的 C0 高出 \+24.8 分的结果，作者在 242 道问题上用两个提取器模型（gemini-2.5-flash-lite 和 gemini-2.5-flash）重跑了三种写入标准（C0、C2 与提示 P）。所有记忆、答案与评分均按预注册协议重做并经独立审查。主要对比——C2 优势在两提取器间的变化——为 −4.1 点（95% 区间 −12.3 至 \+4.1），结果不具决定性。次要对比显示 C2 在两种提取器上均优于 C0。该说明公开了运行、来源与分析以便复现。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: the paper directly relates to component choices for agent memory extraction and to evaluation rigor (pre-registration, artifact sharing). Differences: it examines short-form memory selection and prompt criteria rather than constructing persistent structured graph memory, multi-hop retrieval, or entity-resolution strategies central to the thesis. Complementarity: its paired comparison design and reproducibility practices can inform how to evaluate triple-extraction prompts and extractor-model sensitivity when building the thesis's query-aware graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：该论文直接相关于智能体记忆的提取器选择与评估严谨性（预注册、共享工件）。差异：它检验的是短文本记忆的选择和提示标准，而非论文关注的持久化结构化图记忆、多跳检索或实体解析策略。互补性：其配对比较设计与可复现性做法可为论文在构建查询感知图记忆时评估三元组提取提示与提取器模型敏感性提供方法借鉴。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this note as a methodological caution: extractor prompt effects can be model-dependent, and pre-registered paired runs with artifact publication increase interpretability. Do not overgeneralize results from two closely related Gemini variants to other families.

**中文**

将此说明定位为方法论提醒：提取提示的效果可能依赖于具体模型，采用预注册的配对运行并公开工件可提高可解释性。不要把两种密切相关的 Gemini 变体的结论随意外推到其他模型家族。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt its paired experimental design: run the same writing criteria across multiple extractor models and report effect sizes with confidence intervals. Pre-register the interaction protocol and publish raw rows, provenance and reviewer artifacts. For the thesis, extend this by evaluating the extractors' outputs as triple-extraction inputs and measuring downstream impacts on graph coherence and multi-hop QA.

**中文**

采纳其配对实验设计：在多种提取器模型上运行相同写入标准并报告效应量与置信区间。预注册交互协议并公开原始行、来源与审查工件。对于论文，可在此基础上把提取器的输出作为三元组提取输入并评估其对图结构一致性与多跳问答的下游影响。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend the paired comparison to extractor models used for triple extraction and to a broader model family; isolate extractor strength by controlling inference settings and readers; measure consequences on graph quality, bridge-entity recovery, and multi-hop retrieval accuracy.

**中文**

将配对比较扩展到用于三元组提取的提取器并涵盖更广的模型家族；通过控制推理设定与读取器来分离提取器能力；衡量其对图质量、桥接实体恢复与多跳检索准确性的影响。
