### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses forgetting in coding agents and limitations of prior memory systems that use top-k similarity selection, ignore token costs, and do not track fact validity. It frames agent memory as an online, token-budgeted selection problem and introduces Kanerva, a local memory layer that operates without an LLM in the loop. Kanerva scores candidates with a calibrated relevance model over retrieval, item and context features, expands candidates via neighbouring turns, separates an item's validity from usefulness, and enforces a hard token budget with a per-query certificate and a silence gate when the store cannot answer. The authors give approximation and regret bounds and report empirical gains on repository-based tests across budgets and languages.

**中文**

论文针对编码智能体存在的记忆丢失问题，以及现有记忆系统采用相似度top-k、忽略令牌成本且不建模事实有效性等限制。作者将智能体记忆表述为在线的令牌预算选择问题，提出了 Kanerva —— 一个无需在回路中调用大模型的本地记忆层。Kanerva 用经校准的相关性模型对候选项（基于检索、条目和上下文特征）打分，通过邻近回合扩展候选、将条目的有效性与有用性分离，并在硬令牌预算下选择，同时提供每次查询的证书与在存储无法回答时的沉默门控。文中给出近似和遗憾界，并在跨语言仓库测试中报告了经验性提升。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: Kanerva and the thesis both target persistent agent memory and practical issues of staleness, token budgets, and selection strategies for context. Differences: Kanerva is a flat, unstructured local store (no structured keys or graph construction) and operates without an LLM in its selection loop; it does not address entity resolution, multi-hop path traversal, or knowledge-graph assembly. Complementarity: Kanerva's token-budgeted selection, validity modeling, and theoretical guarantees could be adapted to selecting nodes/subgraphs or triples under prompt budget constraints and to mechanisms for suppressing stale graph facts in the proposed graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：Kanerva 与本论文都关注持久化智能体记忆以及陈旧信息、令牌预算和上下文选择策略等实际问题。差异：Kanerva 是扁平的无结构本地存储（无结构键或图构建），在选择过程中不依赖大模型；它不涉及实体解析、多跳路径遍历或知识图谱的增量组装。互补性：Kanerva 的令牌预算选择、有效性建模和理论保证可用于在提示预算限制下选择图节点/子图或三元组，并可为抑制图记忆中陈旧事实提供机制。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position Kanerva as prior work on budgeted, validity-aware memory layers rather than on structured or graph-based memory. Emphasize its theoretical selection guarantees and empirical metrics for staleness, which are useful comparisons for any persistent-memory claim in the thesis.

**中文**

将 Kanerva 表述为针对预算化、有效性感知记忆层的相关工作，而非结构化或基于图的记忆研究。强调其选择性理论保证和衡量陈旧性的经验指标，这些对于论文中关于持久记忆的论断是有价值的对照。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adapting Kanerva's calibrated relevance scoring and hard token-budget selection to rank candidate triples, nodes, or subgraphs before assembling LLM context; use its separate validity score to decay or suppress low-confidence edges in the graph. Evaluate stale-injection rates and recall under varying token budgets and report per-query certificates or silence rates as verifiability metrics.

**中文**

可考虑将 Kanerva 的经校准相关性打分和硬令牌预算选择用于在构建 LLM 上下文前对候选三元组、节点或子图排序；利用其独立的有效性分数来衰减或抑制图中低置信边。评估不同令牌预算下的陈旧注入率和召回率，并将每查询的证书或沉默率作为可证实性指标报告。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate Kanerva-style token-budgeted selection with an incrementally assembled graph memory: select compact subgraphs or frontier triples under token constraints. Extend the validity model to inform entity-resolution decisions and edge confidence, and test the integrated system on MultiHop-RAG to measure multi-hop retrieval and QA improvements.

**中文**

将 Kanerva 式的令牌预算选择与增量组装的图记忆集成：在令牌约束下选择紧凑子图或前沿三元组。将有效性模型扩展用于指导实体解析和边置信度，并在 MultiHop-RAG 上测试整合系统以衡量多跳检索与问答的改进。
