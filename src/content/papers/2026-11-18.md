### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper presents an Agentic AI framework for academic question answering that combines an agent orchestrator, intent routing, query rewriting, hybrid (lexical \+ character) retrieval, reranking, memory, policy guardrails, a generator, and a verifier to improve grounded answers. Using Design Science Research, the authors implemented a prototype and evaluated it on 12 synthetic academic-policy documents and 216 questions across common university services. Reported retrieval metrics show substantial gains for Agentic RAG over a baseline RAG, attributed mainly to intent-aware query expansion and the hybrid retrieval strategy. The authors note the need for field evaluation on an institutional corpus before deployment.

**中文**

该论文提出一个用于学术问答的智能体式框架，集成了智能体编排、意图路由、查询重写、混合（词汇\+字符）检索、重排序、记忆、策略护栏、生成器和验证器，以提高答案的可溯源性与准确性。作者采用设计科学研究方法实现原型，并在12份合成学术政策文档和216个问题上评估。报告的检索指标显示 Agentic RAG 相较基线 RAG 有显著提升，主要归因于意图感知的查询扩展和混合检索策略。作者指出在正式部署前仍需使用机构语料做实地评估。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both explore Agentic RAG architectures, agent orchestration, memory components, and grounding/verifier mechanisms. Differences: this paper focuses on a practical academic QA application with hybrid retrieval and query expansion, but does not describe persistent structured graph memory, explicit knowledge-graph construction, or entity-resolution strategies. Limitations: evaluation uses a small synthetic corpus and does not target multi-hop, bridge-entity retrieval or the richer graph-coherence metrics central to the thesis. Complementarity: the paper's intent-aware query rewriting, hybrid retrieval, and verifier design could be incorporated as components within a graph-based Agentic RAG system.

**中文**

重合点：两者都研究 Agentic RAG 架构、智能体编排、记忆组件以及答案溯源/验证机制。差异：该论文侧重于学术问答的工程实现，强调混合检索与查询扩展，但未描述持久化的结构化图记忆、显式的知识图谱构建或实体解析策略。局限性：评估基于小规模合成语料，未针对论文关注的多跳检索、桥接实体识别或图一致性等指标。互补性：文章关于意图感知查询重写、混合检索和验证器的设计可被集成到基于图的 Agentic RAG 系统中。

When evaluating, reproduce comparable retrieval metrics (Hit@1, Recall@3, MRR) for the retrieval component, but extend evaluation to multi-hop QA, bridge-entity identification, graph coherence, and end-to-end latency to capture the thesis' central claims.

Also evaluate whether intent-aware query expansion and hybrid retrieval remain beneficial when retrieval operates over evolving graph-backed indexes and whether verifier signals can guide graph updates.

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper presents RagHarnessDebugger, a framework that records and audits every repair decision made by agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems in contract inference tasks. Using a Jev-guided harness, the debugger verifies trace integrity, ensures validations are applied to the correct candidates, and determines which answers are reachable under the acceptance rule. The authors apply the debugger to ContractNLI non-disclosure agreements with two reader models and a replication set, exposing large gaps between available and accepted repairs and revealing hidden failure modes. They report modest accuracy improvements over a BM25-only baseline after revising the harness, and emphasize the debugger's value for visibility, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement of RAG decision policies.

**中文**

本文提出 RagHarnessDebugger 框架，用于记录并审计智能体驱动的检索增强生成（RAG）系统在合同推断任务中的每一次修复决策。基于 Jev 的测试夹具，调试器检查执行轨迹完整性、验证是否正确应用于相应候选项，并判定在接受规则下哪些答案可达。作者在 ContractNLI 的保密协议集上使用两种 reader 模型及复制集，揭示了可用修复与被接受修复之间的显著缺口并暴露隐蔽的失败模式。修订夹具后，相较于仅用 BM25 的基线有小幅提升，论文强调了该调试器在决策可视性、根因分析与持续改进上的价值。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both the thesis and this paper focus on agentic RAG systems and on making repair/decision behavior more verifiable and debuggable. Differences/limitations: RagHarnessDebugger addresses auditing and repair traceability in a contract-inference domain and does not propose or evaluate persistent structured graph memory, triple extraction, entity resolution, or graph traversal methods. Complementarity: the debugger could be adapted to log and audit graph-construction events, entity-resolution decisions, and acceptance criteria in the thesis's graph-memory system to diagnose lost reasoning paths and to measure acceptance gaps for repairs.

**中文**

重叠点：论文与本论文题目都关注智能体驱动的 RAG 系统，并致力于提高修复/决策行为的可验证性与可调试性。差异/局限：RagHarnessDebugger 聚焦合同推断领域的审计与修复轨迹可追溯性，未提出或评估持久的结构化图记忆、三元组抽取、实体消解或图遍历方法。互补性：该调试器可改造为记录并审计图构建事件与实体消解决策，以及接受准则，从而帮助诊断丢失的推理路径并测量修复的接受缺口。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this paper in sections on verification, evaluation methodology, and explainability for agentic RAG. Use its terminology and metrics (trace integrity, acceptance gap, replay-enabled root-cause analysis) when motivating the need for auditability in graph-memory experiments.

**中文**

在关于智能体 RAG 的验证、评估方法和可解释性部分引用该工作。借用其术语和指标（轨迹完整性、接受缺口、回放驱动的根因分析）来论证在图记忆实验中提供审计能力的必要性。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adapt the harness to record graph-specific events: triple extraction outputs, node/edge insertions, entity-resolution merges, confidence scores, and path-traversal decisions. Use the debugger's replay and labeling workflow to trace when and why multi-hop paths break (e.g., due to missed merges) and to quantify an acceptance gap for repairs on MultiHop-RAG. Also measure the debugger-induced overhead to account for end-to-end latency trade-offs when auditing structured memory.

**中文**

将该夹具改造为记录图相关事件：三元组抽取结果、节点/边插入、实体消解合并、置信度分数和路径遍历决策。利用调试器的回放与标注流程追踪多跳路径何时及为何断裂（例如由未合并导致），并在 MultiHop-RAG 上量化修复的接受缺口。同时测量调试器带来的开销，以在审计结构化记忆时评估端到端延迟权衡。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend the debugger to validate structured graph memory by auditing node identity merges and edge provenance, and to check that multi-hop answers follow intact graph paths. Generalize the approach beyond contracts (e.g., MultiHop-RAG) and combine Jev-style acceptance checks with entity-resolution diagnostics to prioritize high-impact repairs.

**中文**

将调试器扩展为验证结构化图记忆：审计节点身份合并与边的来源，并检查多跳答案是否沿着完整的图路径生成。将方法推广到合同之外的数据集（例如 MultiHop-RAG），并将 Jev 式的接受检查与实体消解诊断结合，用于优先定位高影响的修复。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper examines the problem of capturing and operationalizing tacit knowledge—expert judgment, routines, and situated experience—that resists formalization. Grounded in the SECI model, it analyzes how naive, advanced, and agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures map onto organizational knowledge-creation phases, surveys enterprise applications across several domains, and critically assesses structural limitations such as hallucination risk, knowledge-graph construction complexity, and evaluation-framework gaps. The work contributes a conceptual synthesis and a research agenda at the intersection of generative AI and organizational epistemology, arguing that RAG variants offer new affordances for scaling tacit-knowledge elicitation while exposing open technical and evaluation challenges.

**中文**

本文考察了难以形式化的隐性知识（专业判断、工作惯例与情景化经验）的捕获与应用问题。以SECI模型为基础，文章分析了朴素、进阶与智能体检索增强生成（RAG）架构如何对应组织知识创造的各阶段，综述了若干行业应用，并批判性地评估了结构性限制，例如幻觉风险、知识图谱构建复杂性与评估框架缺口。该工作在生成式AI与组织认识论交叉处提出概念性综述与研究议程，指出RAG变体虽可扩展隐性知识提取，但仍面临技术与评估上的未决问题。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both the paper and the thesis focus on RAG/agentic RAG and explicitly note graph construction complexity and evaluation gaps, providing high-level motivation for structured, persistent memory. Differences: the paper is a conceptual, enterprise-oriented survey mapping RAG to organizational knowledge processes and does not present the kind of empirical KG-based agentic RAG system, benchmarks, or entity-resolution comparisons central to the thesis. Complementarity: the paper's identification of hallucination, graph-construction, and evaluation limitations helps justify the thesis's empirical agenda and choice of benchmarks and metrics.

**中文**
