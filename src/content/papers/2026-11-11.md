### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper addresses challenges in retrieving evidence from Indian Knowledge Systems caused by transliteration variants, ambiguous names, and relations spread across passages. The authors propose an ontology-guided knowledge graph (KG) pipeline that extracts typed entities and relations with Qwen3-32B and links them to original textual evidence. Retrieval combines sense-aware grounding, bounded graph traversal, lexical anchors, relationship-vector search, and passage reranking. Evaluation on 320 Dhara Bench questions compares standalone KG, single-vector retrieval (SV), and unions (KG\+SV, KG\+SV\+Entity Notes) under BGE and Qwen rerankers. Standalone KG attains the highest Precision@5 and category MRR@20 in several settings (Qwen Multi-hop MRR@20=0.8722 vs 0.7622 for fully reranked KG\+SV). KG is slower (mean 20.374 s) than SV (0.646 s); comparisons are reported as descriptive.

**中文**

本文针对印度知识体系中由音译变体、姓名歧义及关系分布在多段落造成的证据检索难题，提出一套本体引导的知识图谱（KG）管道：使用 Qwen3-32B 提取类型化实体与关系，并将其映射回原始文本证据。检索组件包括语义感知落地（sense-aware grounding）、有界图遍历、词汇锚点、关系向量搜索与段落重排序。基于 Dhara Bench 的 320 个问题，论文在 BGE 与 Qwen 重排序器下比较了独立 KG、单向量检索（SV）及多种并集配置。独立 KG 在 Precision@5 及若干分类的 MRR@20 中表现最好（Qwen 多跳 MRR@20=0.8722，完全重排序的 KG\+SV 为 0.7622）。KG 检索平均耗时较长（20.374 秒 对比 SV 的 0.646 秒）；所有比较为描述性报告。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: Both works focus on knowledge-graph-based retrieval, linking extracted triples to textual evidence, and multi-hop retrieval via graph traversal. The candidate paper also directly addresses name ambiguity and transliteration—issues central to entity resolution in the thesis. Differences: the paper presents a pipeline KG evaluated on Dhara Bench rather than an incrementally assembled graph produced by an LLM-driven agentic RAG; it reports descriptive comparisons and emphasizes precision vs latency trade-offs rather than persistent, query-accumulating graph memory. Complementarity: the paper's sense-aware grounding, lexical anchors, relationship-vector search, and empirical latency measurements are concrete techniques and metrics that could be integrated into or compared with an agentic, persistent graph-memory system.

**中文**

重合点：两者均以知识图谱（KG）为基础进行检索，关注将提取的三元组与原始文本证据关联，并通过图遍历实现多跳检索。候选论文还直接处理姓名歧义与音译问题——这些正是论文中实体消解的核心问题。差异：该论文呈现的是一条在 Dhara Bench 上评估的管道式 KG，而非由智能体驱动的、增量构造的持久图记忆；它以描述性比较并强调精度与延迟的权衡，而不是查询累积的长期图记忆效果。互补性：论文中的语义感知落地、词汇锚点、关系向量检索及其延迟测量，都是可直接纳入或与智能体式持久图记忆系统比较的具体技术与度量。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper studies how language-model agents learn to use file-based persistent memory for long-horizon tasks by making memory control part of the policy via on-policy distillation (OPD). The authors introduce CAMG-II, a suite of agentic-RL environments that provide both native task interfaces and an episode-persistent workspace accessible via ordinary Bash, enabling create/revise/search/reuse file operations as memory. They propose CAMP-II, a jointly trained policy using asynchronous OPD from a frozen teacher with a prior for file operations, and report substantial gains over OPD baselines (CAMP-II achieves 2.97× the gain of the strongest OPD baseline). The learned memory behavior generalizes to two external benchmarks.

**中文**

该论文研究如何通过将记忆控制纳入策略并使用在线策略蒸馏（OPD），使语言模型智能体利用基于文件的持久记忆来完成长时任务。作者提出了 CAMG-II，一套智能体-RL 环境，除了原生任务接口外还提供基于 Bash 的会话持久工作区，允许以文件创建/修改/检索/重用的方式将文件作为记忆使用。论文提出 CAMP-II，通过异步 OPD 从带有文件操作先验的冻结教师联合训练单一策略，较强的 OPD 基线获得显著增益（CAMP-II 达到 2.97 倍增益）。所学记忆行为对两个外部基准显示出泛化性。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works address persistent memory for agentic systems and the policy-level decision of what to store and when. Difference: this paper focuses on file-based memory and on-policy distillation in agentic-RL environments, whereas the thesis targets graph memory / knowledge graphs and entity-resolution for multi-hop retrieval and QA. Limitation: CAMP-II does not evaluate structured graph writes or entity-resolution trade-offs relevant to the thesis. Complementarity: OPD and the CAMG-II training methodology could inform learning when and how an agent should write triples or paths into an evolving graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：两者都关注智能体系统的持久记忆以及在策略层面上决定何时和存储什么。差异：该论文侧重于基于文件的记忆和在智能体-RL 环境中使用在线策略蒸馏，而本论文聚焦于基于知识图谱（KG）的图记忆及实体解析以支持多跳检索与问答。局限性：CAMP-II 未评估结构化的图写入或与实体解析相关的权衡，这正是论文的核心问题。互补性：OPD 与 CAMG-II 的训练方法可为学习何时以及如何将三元组或推理路径写入增长中的图记忆提供可借鉴的思路。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper in related work as a memory-control and training-methods contribution rather than a structured-memory (KG) solution. Emphasize its methodological lesson that jointly training memory policies with OPD and providing realistic persistent interfaces (file workspace) can improve long-horizon behavior and generalization.

**中文**

在相关工作中将此文归为记忆控制与训练方法类贡献，而非结构化记忆（KG）解决方案。强调其方法论启示：用 OPD 联合训练记忆策略并提供现实的持久接口（文件工作区）可提升长时任务行为与泛化能力。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adapting OPD-style training to learn a graph-write/read policy: treat graph write, read, and compaction as explicit actions in CAMG-II–like environments and distill a teacher that knows graph-operation primitives. Evaluate learned policies on MultiHop-RAG by measuring whether learned policies improve multi-hop retrieval accuracy, bridge-entity identification, and end-to-end latency compared with heuristic or embedding-based write policies.

**中文**

考虑将 OPD 风格的训练用于学习图写入/读取策略：将图的写入、读取和压缩作为显式动作，构造类似 CAMG-II 的环境，并从熟悉图操作原语的教师进行蒸馏。用 MultiHop-RAG 评估所学策略，比较其是否在多跳检索准确率、桥接实体识别和端到端延迟上优于基于启发式或嵌入的写入策略。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend CAMG-II/CAMP-II to include structured graph operations (create node/edge, merge entities, traverse paths) and train graph-memory policies with OPD. Study transfer between file-based and graph-based persistent interfaces and measure impacts on entity-resolution, graph coherence, and multi-hop QA performance.

**中文**

将 CAMG-II/CAMP-II 扩展为包含结构化图操作（创建节点/边、实体合并、路径遍历），并用 OPD 训练图记忆策略。研究文件化接口与图结构化接口之间的迁移，并衡量其对实体解析、图连贯性和多跳问答性能的影响。
