### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper addresses multi-hop question answering over Wikipedia by replacing open-ended LLM-based graph construction with a controlled, local extraction pipeline. It uses a GLiNER2-based schema-guided extractor to populate a corpus-specific knowledge graph (KG), discovers missing relation types from uncovered entity pairs and injects triples post hoc to avoid repeated corpus-wide extraction. Candidate passages are expanded with Personalized PageRank, reranked by a local cross-encoder, and selected via Graph-Path, a bounded topology-aware passage selection method. On 1,000 questions from MuSiQue, 2WikiMultihopQA, and HotpotQA the authors report improved F1 relative to a BGE-based HippoRAG2 baseline; adaptive expansion increases relation coverage and recall. The paper concludes that controlled schema expansion and topology-aware selection reduce dependence on LLM-based KG construction while leaving final answer generation API-based.

**中文**

### Thesis relevance

**English**
