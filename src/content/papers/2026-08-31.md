### Research synopsis

**English**

This theoretical paper examines the common default that, when persistent agent memory contains conflicting records, the newer record supersedes the older. The author identifies three presuppositions underlying that rule: (1) a key that determines which records are in conflict, (2) that recorded write order reflects when facts were true (transaction time vs valid time), and (3) that newer records are at least as trustworthy as older ones. The paper argues these presuppositions are under-justified, critiques the shift toward retaining both records as merely relocating the decision to read-time, and enumerates nine empirical studies and a reporting convention that would help resolve open questions. No experiments are reported.

**中文**

本文为一篇理论性讨论，审视在持久化智能体记忆中遇到冲突记录时普遍采用的新近记录优先规则。作者指出该规则基于三项预设： (1) 存在可识别冲突记录的键，(2) 写入顺序反映事实成立顺序（事务时间 vs 有效时间），(3) 新近记录至少与旧记录同样可信。文章认为这些预设证据不足，批评保留双重记录只是将决策转移到读时，并列举了九项可解决开放问题的实证研究和一项报告约定。文中不包含实验数据。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: the paper addresses fundamental policies for persistent agent memory, including provenance, temporal semantics, and whether to supersede or retain prior records—issues directly relevant to graph memory update rules and entity resolution policies in the thesis. Differences: it is a conceptual and literature-analytic argument without empirical evaluation and it does not propose or evaluate structured knowledge-graph construction, multi-hop retrieval, or local LLM-driven agentic RAG. Complementarity: its analysis of write-time vs read-time decisions and recommended empirical studies can inform the thesis's update semantics, evaluation design, and reporting conventions.

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Implement and compare three memory-update regimes in the thesis system: (a) newest-wins supersession, (b) retain-all with read-time adjudication, and (c) hybrid rules keyed by conflict identifier and valid/transaction time. Measure effects on graph coherence, bridge-entity fragmentation, multi-hop retrieval accuracy, and end-to-end latency. Instrument and report provenance metadata (write time, extraction confidence) so read-time policies can be objectively evaluated; follow the paper's suggested studies and reporting convention as a checklist.

**中文**

在论文系统中实现并比较三类记忆更新策略：(a) 新近优先覆盖，(b) 全部保留并在读时裁决，(c) 基于冲突键与有效/事务时间的混合规则。度量这些策略对图一致性、桥实体碎片化、多跳检索准确性和端到端延迟的影响。记录并上报溯源元数据（写入时间、抽取置信度），以便对读时策略进行客观评估；可将该文提出的实证研究和报告约定作为实现检查表。
