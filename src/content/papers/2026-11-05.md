### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper addresses explainability and reliability limits of task-specific stress detectors in safety-critical operations. It proposes a hybrid human–agentic framework combining In-Context Learning (ICL) for few-shot, context-aware stress detection from physiological and contextual signals with a GraphRAG module that constructs a domain-specific knowledge graph to ground explanations and intervention recommendations. Validated in a five-month Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) deployment, the ICL predictor attains an F1 of 88.37% with 256 examples, and expert evaluation reports GraphRAG outputs improve accuracy, usability, and trustworthiness versus LLM-only baselines. The work emphasizes multi-hop, evidence-grounded recommendations and reduced hallucination through KG grounding.

**中文**

本文针对安全关键环境中以往应激检测方法可解释性与可靠性不足的问题。提出一个混合人-智能体框架：使用 ICL 进行少样本、情境感知的应激检测，融合生理信号与多情境信息；并用 GraphRAG 模块构建领域专用知识图谱（KG）以为解释与干预建议提供支撑。基于五个月的船舶交通服务（VTS）现场部署，ICL 预测器在 256 个示例下达到 F1=88.37%，专家评估表明 GraphRAG 增强的输出在准确性、可用性和可信度上优于仅 LLM 基线。工作强调基于证据的多跳推理与通过 KG 约束降低幻觉。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses multi-hop retrieval for RAG-style tasks, observing that scoring evidence independently against the original question can miss items whose relevance appears only after intermediate facts are discovered. It proposes QueryFlow, a graph-retrieval architecture that recurrently integrates query-conditioned summaries of accumulated graph context into a shared query state. That query state then controls propagation and relation features for subsequent retrieval steps, coupling the evolution of evidence representations with query conditions. On four multi-hop QA datasets the authors report gains in macro Recall@10, Full Recall@10, and LLM-Acc of 4.98, 8.40, and 8.0 percentage points respectively over representative baselines.

**中文**

论文针对用于检索增强生成（RAG）任务的多跳检索问题，指出仅用原始问题独立评分证据会错过那些在发现中间事实后才显现相关性的证据单元。作者提出 QueryFlow，一种图检索架构，通过将查询条件下的图上下文摘要循环地整合到共享查询状态中，使查询状态控制后续传播和关系特征，从而耦合证据表示与查询条件的演化。在四个多跳问答数据集上，作者报告在 macro Recall@10、Full Recall@10 和 LLM-Acc 上比分 baselines 分别提升 4.98、8.40 和 8.0 个百分点。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: QueryFlow shares the thesis' focus on graph-structured retrieval for multi-hop evidence recovery and on adapting retrieval using intermediate context. Difference: QueryFlow emphasizes query-conditioned propagation and closed-dataset graph retrievers, while the thesis centers on an agentic RAG pipeline with incrementally constructed, persistent graph memory and explicit entity-resolution strategies. Complementarity: QueryFlow's recurrent query-state mechanism could be adapted to control how an incrementally assembled graph memory is queried and updated within the thesis' agentic loop.

**中文**

重合点：QueryFlow 与本论文都关注用于多跳证据恢复的图结构检索，以及利用中间上下文调整检索。差异：QueryFlow 强调基于查询的传播机制和在固定数据集上的图检索器，而本论文侧重于带有增量构建的持久图记忆的智能体式 RAG 流程，并强调实体消解策略。互补性：QueryFlow 的循环查询状态机制可以被改造为在论文的智能体循环中控制如何查询和更新增量组装的图记忆。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position QueryFlow as a query-aware graph-retrieval technique rather than an agent-memory solution. When discussing related work, highlight its explicit coupling of query state and propagation as a distinct design point among graph-based retrievers.

**中文**

将 QueryFlow 定位为一种查询感知的图检索技术，而非智能体记忆方案。在相关工作中强调其将查询状态与传播耦合的设计点，作为图检索方法谱系中的区分点。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider integrating QueryFlow's recurrent query-state updates as a retrieval controller within the thesis' incremental graph memory so that new triples and edges are retrieved with context-aware queries. Empirically compare QueryFlow-style propagation to the thesis' existing graph traversal for bridge-entity identification and multi-hop QA, measuring recall, bridge-entity F1, end-to-end latency, and how performance changes as graph memory accumulates. Also assess whether query-conditioned summaries aid or complicate entity-resolution heuristics.

**中文**

考虑将 QueryFlow 的循环查询状态更新作为检索控制器整合进论文的增量图记忆，使新增三元组和边能通过上下文感知的查询被检索。实证上将 QueryFlow 风格的传播与论文现有的图遍历方法在桥接实体识别和多跳问答上进行对比，测量召回、桥接实体 F1、端到端延迟，以及随图记忆积累性能的变化。同时评估查询条件化摘要是否有助于或使实体消解启发式更复杂。

### Future directions

**English**

Explore integrating query-conditioned propagation with low-latency embedding-based matching to trade off precision and latency.

**中文**

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper studies GraphRAG-style retrieval when the underlying knowledge graph construction changes. It introduces ArcRAG, which at inference time induces a query-local graph interface (node labels, relation descriptors, properties reachable from named entities), plans multi-hop traversals against that local interface, and executes and repairs traversals as Cypher. The authors build each corpus (MuSiQue, HotpotQA, TriviaQA) as two graph families—schema-guided and open‑IE—and evaluate a fixed retriever configuration across them, ablating stages and budgets. They report that query-time interface induction keeps a construction-agnostic retriever effective across construction changes, while absolute accuracy remains shaped by the construction.

**中文**

本文研究当底层知识图谱构建发生变化时的 GraphRAG 风格检索的可移植性。作者提出 ArcRAG：在推理时诱导查询感知图接口（问题命名实体可到达的节点标签、关系描述和属性键），基于该接口规划多跳遍历，并以 Cypher 执行与修复遍历。研究中将 MuSiQue、HotpotQA、TriviaQA 每个语料分别构建为 schema-guided 与 open‑IE 两类图，在固定检索器配置下进行评估并做阶段与预算消融。作者报告称，查询时接口诱导可使检索器在构建变化时保持有效，但构建方式仍影响绝对准确率。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: ArcRAG addresses retrieval over different graph constructions and a query-local interface, which is directly relevant to designing retrieval methods that do not depend on a specific graph schema. Difference/limitation: ArcRAG, as presented, focuses on corpus-derived static graphs and cross-construction portability rather than on incrementally assembled, persistent graph memory produced by agent interactions. Complementarity: its query-time interface induction and Cypher-based planning/repair techniques could be adapted as a retrieval/execution layer within the thesis's agentic system to decouple retrieval from how the graph memory was assembled.

**中文**

重合点：ArcRAG 研究在不同图构建下的检索以及查询感知图接口，这与设计不依赖特定图模式的检索方法直接相关。不同/限制：ArcRAG 着重于从语料构建的静态图及构建可移植性，而非由智能体交互增量组装的持久图记忆。互补性：其查询时接口诱导和基于 Cypher 的规划/修复方法可被借用为论文所述智能体系统中的检索/执行层，以弱化检索对图记忆构建细节的依赖。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position ArcRAG as a study of construction-agnostic retrieval: it provides evidence and a concrete mechanism (query-local interface) showing retrieval can be decoupled from graph construction, which is useful when arguing for modular agent architectures. Contrast its corpus-centric graphs with the thesis's agent-driven, incremental graph memory to clarify contributions.

**中文**

将 ArcRAG 表述为对构建无关检索的研究：其提出的查询感知接口为将检索与图构建解耦提供了证据和具体机制，可支持主张模块化智能体架构。将其语料中心的图与论文中智能体驱动的增量图记忆对比以澄清各自贡献。

**English**

Reuse the paper's Cypher planning-and-repair pattern as an execution layer for multi-hop traversals, and ablate retry/pass budgets and construction variants when evaluating agentic graph memory. Measure portability of retrieval when the incremental assembly strategy (triple extraction, entity resolution) changes.

**中文**

### Future directions

**English**

Investigate integrating entity-resolution strategies used in the thesis to preserve graph coherence across heterogeneous construction styles.

**中文**

Consider adopting the paper's evidence-unit standardization and provenance controls as concrete graph-quality checks when evaluating incremental graph memory. The AHP-based user-weighting approach could be integrated to produce query- or task-aware edge weighting in a persistent graph. However, extend evaluation beyond 30 queries and explicit manual verification by testing automated entity-resolution and accumulation behaviors under realistic interaction sequences.

**中文**

可借鉴论文的证据单元标准化和来源控制，作为评估增量图记忆时的具体图质量检查机制。AHP 的用户加权方法可用于生成查询或任务感知的边权重以纳入持久化图记忆。但应将评估规模扩展到超过 30 条查询，并在现实交互序列下测试自动化的实体解析与记忆累积行为，而非仅依赖人工核验。

### Future directions

**English**

Follow-ups could integrate NED-GraphRAG's provenance and user-weighting into an agentic RAG pipeline that incrementally writes triples during interactions, and then measure effects on multi-hop retrieval and verifiability. Another direction is to evaluate automated entity-resolution strategies on their curated graph to quantify fragmentation risk and compare with embedding- or LLM-based resolution approaches.

**中文**

后续工作可以将 NED-GraphRAG 的证据可溯源与用户加权机制整合进能在交互中增量写入三元组的智能体 RAG 流程，进而测量其对多跳检索与可验证性的影响。另一个方向是在其已整理的图谱上评估自动实体解析策略，以量化实体碎片化风险并与基于嵌入或以 LLM 为裁判的解析方法进行比较。
