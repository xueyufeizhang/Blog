### Research synopsis

**English**

This poster describes a pipeline that converts flat Europeana cultural-heritage records into a FAIR Digital Object (FDO)-compliant knowledge graph structured with CIDOC-CRM. The authors use an LLM to decide the PID/literal boundary before entity linking and to route resolvable values to external authorities (Getty AAT, Wikidata, VIAF, PeriodO). On 637 archaeological records they resolve 58.5% of values left as plain text and merge 176 surface forms into 33 shared entities, of which 17 were judged correct in manual review. The authors note that graph connectivity was not superior to string matching, and that the FDO advantage is typed, resolvable nodes.

**中文**

该海报介绍了一个管道，将扁平的 Europeana 文化遗产记录转换为符合 FAIR 数字对象（FDO）规范并以 CIDOC-CRM 建模的知识图谱（KG）。作者使用 LLM 在实体链接前判定 PID/字面值边界，并将可解析的值路由到外部权威库（Getty AAT、Wikidata、VIAF、PeriodO）。在 637 条考古记录上，他们解析了 58.5% 的原为纯文本的值，并将 176 个表面形式合并为 33 个共享实体，其中手工审查认为 17 个是正确的。作者指出图的连通性并未优于字符串匹配，FDO 的区别在于每个节点都有类型且可解析。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both the thesis and this work focus on automated construction and entity resolution for knowledge graphs and use LLMs to assist metadata normalization and routing to authority identifiers. Differences/limitations: this paper targets cultural-heritage metadata and FDO/CIDOC-CRM modeling rather than agentic RAG, persistent graph memory assembled from agent interactions, or multi-hop QA evaluation. Complementarity: the LLM-based PID/literal boundary classifier and routing to external vocabularies are operational techniques the thesis could reuse for grounding nodes and improving entity resolution in the evolving graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：本论文与该工作都关注知识图谱的自动构建与实体消歧，且均使用 LLM 协助元数据规范化和路由到权威标识符。差异/局限：该论文面向文化遗产元数据和 FDO/CIDOC-CRM 建模，而非基于智能体的 RAG、由智能体交互增量组装的持久图记忆或多跳问答评估。互补性：其基于 LLM 的 PID/字面值边界判别以及路由至外部词表的做法，可作为本论文在对图记忆中节点落地和提高实体解析精度时的可重用技术。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper as domain-focused KG construction that contributes practical LLM-assisted grounding and PID enforcement; emphasize its empirical reporting of resolvable-value rates and manual precision. Note the candid observation that connectivity gains were limited, which is informative when juxtaposing structured graph memory versus string/embedding methods.

**中文**

将此工作定位为面向领域的知识图谱构建研究，提供实用的 LLM 辅助落地与 PID 强制策略；重视其对可解析值比例和人工精度的实证报告。注意其坦率指出图连通性并未带来显著优势，这一结论在比较结构化图记忆与字符串/向量方法时具有参考价值。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adopting the paper's LLM-based PID/literal classifier as a preprocessing step before triple extraction in the thesis pipeline. Reuse their evaluation metrics (fraction of resolvable values, counts of merged surface forms, and manual correctness on merged entities) as lightweight diagnostics when comparing embedding-based versus LLM-as-judge entity resolution. If possible, augment their manual review with larger annotated ground truth to better measure precision/recall trade-offs.

**中文**

可将该论文的基于 LLM 的 PID/字面值分类器作为三元组抽取前的预处理步骤加入论文管道。借用其评估指标（可解析值比例、合并表面形式数量以及合并后实体的人工正确率）作为对比低延迟嵌入相似性与高精度 LLM 判定实体解析策略的诊断手段。若可行，应以更大规模的标注金标准补充人工审查，以更可靠地衡量精度/召回权衡。

### Future directions

**English**

Apply the PID/literal boundary and authority-routing approach to open-domain or QA-oriented corpora (e.g., datasets used by the thesis) to test transferability. Combine their grounding methods with the thesis's graph memory and agentic workflows to measure effects on multi-hop retrieval and verifiability.

**中文**

## 02 · [Knowledge Graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Schema Matching](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.08686)

  <h3>Reading verdict</h3>

**English**

Recommended for a skim: the paper offers transferable retrieval, graph-traversal, and reranking techniques and an instruction-tuning approach to exclude misleading KG context, but it targets external-KG schema matching rather than the thesis's incremental, agentic graph memory and entity-resolution trade-offs.

**中文**

推荐略读：该文提供了可迁移的混合检索、图遍历与重排序技术，以及用于排除误导性KG上下文的指令调优思路，但其面向外部KG的模式匹配任务不同于本论文关注的增量、智能体驱动的图记忆与实体解析权衡。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses failures of traditional similarity-based schema matching and LLM hallucination by introducing KG-RAG4SM, a Knowledge Graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation approach for schema matching. It combines multiple retrieval mechanisms—LLM-based, vector-based, graph-traversal-based, and hybrid—plus advanced reranking to extract relevant subgraphs from large external KGs without retraining. The authors report substantial precision and F1 improvements on MIMIC and Synthea, claim scalability across commonsense and domain KGs, and propose an instruction-tuning knowledge-exclusion component to avoid misleading KG context. Empirical analysis and case studies are used to argue that the approach mitigates hallucination in schema-matching tasks.

**中文**

本文针对传统相似度基础的模式匹配在语义歧义和域特定映射上的失败及大模型（LLM）产生幻觉的问题，提出了KG-RAG4SM：一种基于知识图谱（KG）的检索增强生成（RAG）方法用于模式匹配。该方法结合了多种检索机制——基于LLM、基于向量、基于图遍历和混合检索——并配以先进的重排序策略，从大型外部KG中提取相关子图且无需重训练。作者在MIMIC和Synthea数据集上报告了显著的精确率和F1提升，声称可在常识与领域KG间扩展，并通过指令调优的知识排除组件减少有害的KG上下文干扰。实证分析与案例研究用于说明该方法在模式匹配中降低幻觉现象。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works integrate knowledge graphs with RAG-like retrieval and emphasize extracting relevant subgraphs or relations to improve task accuracy and reduce hallucination. Differences: this paper focuses on using large external KGs for one-shot schema matching, whereas the thesis studies an incrementally assembled local graph memory created from the system's own interactions and targets multi-hop QA with agentic RAG. Limitations relative to the thesis: KG-RAG4SM does not appear to address persistent, query-accumulating graph memory, entity-resolution trade-offs across interactions, or comparisons among agentic paradigms and latency on consumer hardware. Complementarity: its hybrid retrieval, graph-traversal extraction, reranking, and instruction-tuning knowledge-exclusion ideas could be adapted to improve subgraph selection and noise mitigation in an incremental graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：两者都将知识图谱与类似RAG的检索结合，强调从KG中提取相关子图或关系以提高任务准确性并减少幻觉。差异：该论文侧重使用大型外部KG进行一次性模式匹配，而本论文题目研究的是从系统自身交互逐步构建的本地增量图记忆，并针对带有智能体的多跳问答进行设计。相对于本论文的局限性：KG-RAG4SM 似乎未涉及持久的、随查询累积的图记忆、跨交互的实体解析权衡，或在消费级硬件上对智能体范式与延迟的比较。互补性：其混合检索、图遍历子图提取、重排序和指令调优的知识排除思路可用于改进增量图记忆中的子图选择与噪声缓解。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position KG-RAG4SM as a methods-focused RAG paper that emphasizes diverse retrieval modalities and reranking to combat KG noise and LLM hallucination. Note that its external-KG, single-task framing contrasts with work on persistent local graph memory; highlight its instruction-tuning knowledge-exclusion as a noteworthy pattern for preventing over-reliance on noisy context.

**中文**

将KG-RAG4SM 定位为方法导向的RAG工作，强调多样化检索模式与重排序以对抗KG噪声和LLM幻觉。注意其外部KG、单任务的设置与持久本地图记忆研究形成对比；其指令调优的知识排除是防止过度依赖噪声上下文的值得关注的做法。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adapting the paper's hybrid retrieval and graph-traversal subgraph extraction techniques to fetch candidate neighborhoods from the thesis's local graph memory, followed by a reranker to improve bridge-entity selection. The instruction-tuning knowledge-exclusion component could be repurposed to avoid using low-confidence triples during answer generation. Empirically, run ablations comparing LLM-based, vector-based, and traversal retrieval on MultiHop-RAG, measuring bridge-entity identification, multi-hop QA accuracy, graph coherence, and end-to-end latency.

**中文**

可考虑将该文的混合检索与图遍历子图提取技术改造用于从论文中的本地图记忆中检索候选邻域，随后使用重排序器改善桥接实体选择。其指令调优的知识排除组件可用于避免在生成答案时使用低置信度三元组。在实证上应做消融实验，比较在 MultiHop-RAG 上的 LLM 驱动、向量驱动与遍历检索，测量桥接实体识别、多跳QA准确率、图一致性与端到端延迟。

### Future directions

**English**

Explore integrating external-KG subgraph retrieval and reranking with an incrementally assembled local graph memory, investigating whether external subgraph priors accelerate graph growth and improve multi-hop retrieval. Evaluate applying instruction-tuning knowledge-exclusion to entity-resolution decisions and to suppress noisy triples during long-term memory accumulation.

**中文**

探索将外部KG的子图检索与重排序与增量组装的本地图记忆结合，研究外部子图先验是否能加速图增长并提升多跳检索。评估将指令调优的知识排除应用于实体解析决策以及在长期记忆积累期间抑制噪声三元组的效果。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This review surveys methods that reduce hallucinations in large language models by grounding generation in external sources. It covers dense passage retrieval, retrieval-augmented pretraining, and graph-based RAG approaches that exploit structured facts and multi-hop reasoning over knowledge graphs. The paper discusses evaluation paradigms for truthfulness, faithfulness, context relevance and answer relevance, and it proposes a hybrid architecture that combines vector retrieval with knowledge-graph lookup, assembly of grounded context, citation-aware generation and post-generation verification. Research gaps and practical challenges—such as retrieval errors, outdated knowledge, KG construction cost and latency—are identified and compared across grounding strategies.

**中文**

该综述回顾了通过将生成过程以外部知识为依据来减少大型语言模型幻觉的方法。内容包括稠密段落检索、检索增强预训练以及利用知识图谱进行多跳推理的基于图的RAG方法。论文讨论了用于真值性、可信性、上下文相关性和答案相关性的评估范式，并提出了一种将向量检索与知识图谱查找相结合的混合架构，包含构建有依据的上下文、带引用的生成及生成后验证。文章还指出了研究空白与实际挑战，如检索错误、知识过时、知识图谱构建成本与延迟。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both address combining检索增强生成（RAG）with knowledge graphs (KGs) to improve factuality, multi-hop reasoning and verifiability. Differences/limitations: the paper is a broad review and does not present empirical results on persistent, incrementally assembled graph memory or on agentic systems that accumulate structured memory across interactions. Complementarity: the survey's taxonomy of grounding strategies, evaluation metrics, and identified challenges can inform the thesis's experimental design, baselines, and evaluation choices.

**中文**

重合点：两者都讨论将检索增强生成（RAG）与知识图谱（KG）结合以提高事实性、多跳推理和可验证性。差异/局限：该综述属于宏观回顾，未提供关于持久、增量构建的图记忆或跨交互累积结构化记忆的智能体系统的实证比较。互补性：综述中对落地策略、评估指标及挑战的分类可为论文的实验设计、基线选择和评估方法提供参考。

**English**

Cite this review when motivating grounding strategies and faithfulness metrics; use its discussion of graph construction cost and latency to justify evaluation dimensions. Frame the thesis as moving beyond survey-level prescriptions to empirical comparisons in agentic settings with persistent graph memory.

**中文**

Implement the proposed hybrid vector\+KG lookup as a baseline and measure KG construction cost and end-to-end latency separately. Use the survey to compile candidate failure modes (retrieval errors, outdated facts) and design targeted ablations around entity resolution and memory accumulation.
