### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses low recall and precision in semantic retrieval for multi-hop question answering by interleaving dense retrieval with traversal over a pre-existing external knowledge graph (Wikidata). An LLM is used to plan graph traversals by generating SPARQL queries; rather than executing queries, the system parses the generated SPARQL to obtain a traversal plan that confirms dense-retrieved passages against a reasoning path. Evaluated on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue, the approach reports improved EM/F1 (62.5/75.1; 73.5/81.0; 37.1/50.0) versus reproduced baselines. The authors also analyze the penalty of executing generated SPARQL (up to 11.4 EM lost) largely due to empty query results.

**中文**

该论文针对多跳问答中语义检索在召回和精度上的不足，提出将密集检索与基于外部预先存在的知识图谱（Wikidata）的图遍历交错进行。系统用大型语言模型（LLM）生成用于遍历的SPARQL查询，但不是执行这些查询，而是解析生成的SPARQL以获得遍历计划，用以将密集检索到的段落与知识图谱中的推理路径相互确认。在HotpotQA、2WikiMultiHopQA和MuSiQue上的评估显示优于复现的基线，分别达到EM/F1 62.5/75.1、73.5/81.0、37.1/50.0。作者还分析了执行生成SPARQL带来的性能损失（最多11.4 EM），主要因原始查询常返回空结果。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both this paper and the thesis target multi-hop QA improvements by combining text retrieval with knowledge-graph-based reasoning and use LLMs to produce traversal plans. Key difference: the paper relies on a pre-existing, curated external KG (Wikidata) and focuses on single-hop traversal plans parsed from generated SPARQL, whereas the thesis studies an incrementally assembled, persistent graph memory created from the system's own interactions and emphasizes entity resolution and long-term reuse. Limitation relative to the thesis: methods and results assume a large external KG and do not address constructing or resolving fragmented entities in an evolving local graph. Complementarity: the paper's LLM-as-planner and parse-vs-execute analysis can inform planning and execution choices in an agentic system that builds and queries its own graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：该论文与本论文均致力于通过将文本检索与知识图谱推理结合来改进多跳问答，并使用LLM生成遍历计划。主要区别：该论文依赖于预先存在的、人工维护的外部知识图谱（Wikidata），并集中于将生成的SPARQL解析为单跳遍历计划；而本论文研究由系统交互增量构建的持久化图记忆，强调实体解析与长期重用。相对于本论文的局限：该论文假定存在完整外部KG，未处理随交互演化的本地图的构建或实体碎片化问题。互补性：其LLM作为规划器及“解析而非执行”策略的分析，可为构建并查询自主图记忆的智能体在规划与执行选择上提供启发。

### Writing and related work

**English**

The parsing-rather-than-executing SPARQL trick is a practical way to avoid brittle query failures against a KG; reportable gains over strong baselines strengthen the claim that KG confirmation improves precision. Position this work as complementary empirical evidence that KG-guided verification can improve multi-hop retrieval precision when a reliable external KG is available.

**中文**

“解析而非执行”生成的SPARQL是一种避免针对KG发生脆弱性查询失败的实用手段；相对于强基线的性能提升支持了在可用外部KG情形下用KG校验提高精度的观点。可将该工作定位为在存在可靠外部KG时，KG引导的验证能改善多跳检索精度的互补实证。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adopting the paper's LLM-as-planner module to generate traversal plans (SPARQL or plan-structured code) over an incrementally built graph memory, but parse plans to avoid empty-return failures. Empirically compare parsing vs executing generated plans on a constructed local KG, and measure effects on bridge-entity identification, retrieval precision, and end-to-end latency. Also test the paper's interleaving strategy: use dense retrieval to propose seed regions and then apply graph traversal constrained to locally accumulated nodes and edges.

**中文**

可以考虑将该论文的LLM作为规划器模块用于在增量构建的图记忆上生成遍历计划（SPARQL或结构化计划代码），但解析计划以避免空返回失败。可在构建的本地图上实证比较解析与执行生成计划的差异，并衡量其对桥接实体识别、检索精度和端到端延迟的影响。同时测试其交错策略：先用密集检索定位种子区域，再对本地累积的节点与边施加图遍历约束。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend the planner to generate not only traversal queries but also graph-update operations (add triples, confidence weights) so LLM plans both retrieval and memory growth. Evaluate the parse-vs-execute trade-offs when the KG is the agent's own evolving graph and measure how entity-resolution quality affects traversal reliability.

**中文**

将规划器扩展为不仅生成遍历查询，还生成图更新操作（添加三元组、置信权重），以使LLM兼顾检索与记忆增长。在KG为智能体自身演化图的情形下评估解析与执行的权衡，并测量实体解析质量如何影响遍历可靠性。
