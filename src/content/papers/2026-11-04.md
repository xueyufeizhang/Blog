### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses consistency in long-lived agent memory: when an upstream fact changes, dependent beliefs, plans, or actions can remain accepted even though their support no longer holds. It proposes StateGraph, which maintains an evolving state dependency graph reconstructed from interaction histories. The core mechanism is counterfactual verification: candidate edges are tested by asking whether a dependent state would survive the removal of its premise, distinguishing true support dependence from mere semantic relatedness. Revision invalidates a dependent state only when a strict dependency fails and no independent support path remains. The authors introduce StateChangeBench (50 controlled cases) and report improvements in Impact-F1 versus a CUPMem reimplementation and comparable or higher point estimates on public long-term memory benchmarks.

**中文**

论文关注长期智能体记忆的一致性问题：上游事实变化时，依赖于该事实的信念、计划或行为可能仍被接受，但其支持已不存在。作者提出 StateGraph，通过从交互历史中恢复的状态依赖图来维护不断演化的依赖结构。核心机制是反事实验证：对候选边进行检验，询问在移除前提后依赖状态是否仍然成立，从而把支持性依赖与语义相关性区分开来。仅在严格依赖失败且没有独立支持路径存活时才使依赖状态失效。论文同时引入了包含50个对照案例的 StateChangeBench，并在该基准上与 CUPMem 重现比较得到 Impact-F1 的改进，同时在若干公开长期记忆基准上报告了可比或更高的点估计。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works address long-term agent memory and the need to maintain coherent, evolving structured memories. StateGraph's explicit dependency tracking and counterfactual revision directly bear on graph coherence and selective invalidation of stored reasoning paths. Differences: the paper focuses on consistency and selective revision after upstream changes, whereas the thesis centers on building and reusing an incrementally assembled graph memory for multi-hop retrieval, triple extraction, and entity resolution. Limitation: the paper's abstract does not address triple extraction, entity-resolution strategies, or multi-hop QA evaluation on datasets like MultiHop-RAG. Complementarity: integrating dependency-aware revision could help the thesis system avoid reuse of invalidated entities/paths and improve verifiability of multi-hop answers.

**中文**

重合点：两者均关注长期智能体记忆以及维护连贯、可演化的结构化记忆的需求。StateGraph 的显式依赖追踪和反事实修订直接关系到图记忆的一致性与对已存推理路径的选择性作废。差异：该论文聚焦于上游事实变化后的记忆一致性和选择性修订，论文摘要未涉及三元组抽取、实体解析策略或针对 MultiHop-RAG 之类数据集的多跳问答评估，而毕业论文主要关注增量组装的图记忆、多跳检索与实体解析。互补性：将依赖感知修订整合进论文的系统，可帮助避免重用已失效的实体/路径并提升多跳回答的可验证性。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this work when discussing memory coherence, selective forgetting, or verifiability in agentic systems; its counterfactual verification framing is a clear method-level contrast to naive timestamp- or confidence-based revision. Emphasize the distinction between semantic relatedness and support dependence when positioning graph-memory coherence.

**中文**

在讨论记忆一致性、选择性遗忘或智能体系统的可验证性时应引用此工作；其反事实验证的表述在方法上可与简单的时间戳或置信度驳回策略形成鲜明对比。在描述图记忆一致性时应强调语义相关性与支持性依赖的区别。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider integrating counterfactual verification into the thesis graph pipeline to test whether a multi-hop path still holds after removing a premise node or edge; use interaction histories to recover candidate dependency edges as StateGraph does. Evaluate the impact on multi-hop retrieval and bridge-entity reuse by measuring cascade depth and selective invalidation metrics (e.g., Impact-F1) alongside existing QA accuracy and latency measurements. Use StateChangeBench-like controlled cases to simulate cascading failures introduced by entity resolution errors or factual edits.

**中文**

考虑将反事实验证整合到论文的图式管道中，以在移除某个前提节点或边后检验多跳路径是否仍然成立；像 StateGraph 那样从交互历史中恢复候选依赖边。把级联深度和选择性失效指标（如 Impact-F1）与现有的 QA 准确率和延迟度量一起用于评估对多跳检索与桥接实体重用的影响。使用类似 StateChangeBench 的受控案例来模拟由实体解析错误或事实更新引发的级联失效。

### Future directions

**English**

Explore adapting counterfactual tests to operate on weighted, uncertain edges with confidence scores (as in the thesis graph memory) and to run efficiently under local-model latency constraints. Study how entity-resolution errors interact with dependency tracking and whether joint optimization reduces cascade depth.

**中文**

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses noisy context and weak retrieval–generation alignment in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for multi-hop question answering, observing that Apple's Continuous Latent Reasoning (CLaRa) compresses retrieved documents into dense latent tokens at the cost of obscuring inter-document structure. The authors propose GraphCLaRa, a lightweight (~1.3M-parameter) graph-based reranker that builds a query-aware document graph, propagates evidence using a Graph Attention Network (GAT), and selects a compact document subset via an adaptive context-budget head for a frozen CLaRa reader. Evaluated on four single- and multi-hop QA benchmarks, GraphCLaRa improves F1 on three datasets (up to \+6.6) while using fewer documents, and underperforms by 1.1 F1 on a retrieval-saturated benchmark, suggesting graph-guided selection complements latent-memory compression.

**中文**

本文针对检索增强生成（RAG）在多跳问答中存在的上下文噪声和检索—生成对齐弱的问题，指出 Apple 的 Continuous Latent Reasoning（CLaRa）将检索到的文档压缩为密集潜在记忆令牌，但会掩盖文档间的结构关系。作者提出 GraphCLaRa，一个轻量级（约1.3M参数）的基于图的重排序器：构建查询感知的文档图，采用图注意力网络（GAT）传播证据，并通过自适应上下文预算头为冻结的 CLaRa 阅读器选择紧凑的文档子集。在四个单跳与多跳 QA 基准上的评估表明，GraphCLaRa 在三项数据集上提升了 F1（最高 \+6.6），使用更少文档；在一个检索饱和的数据集上落后 1.1 F1，表明图引导选择可补充潜在记忆压缩。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works use graph structure to improve multi-hop QA and evidence selection. Differences: GraphCLaRa is a graph-based reranker applied to a frozen compressed latent-memory reader and does not construct or maintain a persistent, incrementally assembled graph memory nor address entity resolution or agentic behavior. Complementarity: the paper's query-aware document-graph construction and lightweight GAT reranker could be incorporated as a module for selecting candidate evidence before insertion into the thesis's evolving graph memory.

**中文**

重合点：两者都利用图结构改善多跳问答与证据选择。差异：GraphCLaRa 是用于冻结的压缩潜在记忆阅读器的基于图的重排序器，并不构建或维护持久的、增量汇集的图记忆，也未处理实体解析或智能体行为。互补性：其查询感知文档图构建和轻量 GAT 重排序器可作为模块用于在将证据写入论文所述的演化图记忆前筛选候选证据。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position GraphCLaRa as evidence that explicit document-graph propagation can mitigate structural information loss from latent-memory compression. When citing, emphasize its lightweight reranker design and empirical distinction between retrieval-improvable and retrieval-saturated regimes.

**中文**

将 GraphCLaRa 表述为证明显式文档图传播能缓解潜在记忆压缩导致的结构信息损失。引用时强调其轻量重排序器设计及其在检索可改进与检索饱和两类情形中的经验差异。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider reproducing GraphCLaRa's query-aware document-graph \+ GAT reranker as a pre-filter in the thesis pipeline, measuring its impact on downstream graph construction and multi-hop path recovery. Evaluate interaction effects with entity-resolution strategies (embedding-similarity vs. LLM-as-judge), and report per-query document budget, retrieval F1, bridge-entity identification, and end-to-end latency to compare against pure vector RAG and agentic systems without persistent graph memory.

**中文**

### Future directions

**English**

Extend GraphCLaRa toward persistent query-aware graph memory by retaining selected document nodes and propagated edges across interactions, and study how compression-plus-graph selection affects incremental graph coherence and multi-hop retrieval over time. Also investigate integrating entity-resolution into the reranker so selected nodes correspond to canonical entities used in long-term graph memory.

**中文**
