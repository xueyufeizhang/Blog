### Research synopsis

**English**

The system builds 31 daily heterogeneous KG snapshots, selects candidate nodes by keyword matching plus semantic embeddings, filters them by spatial/temporal constraints, and expands a geography-aware evidence subgraph for answer generation. The framework also integrates spatio-temporal summarization and a risk-priority categorization module, and reports metrics for context relevance, subgraph compression, answer relevance, and faithfulness; code is linked in the paper.

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses efficient memory management for LLM-based agents that accumulate long interaction histories. It argues that not only what to store but the perceptual granularity of each past interaction matters. The authors propose Resolution as Attention (RAA): they render past interactions as images and dynamically adjust each interaction's visual resolution according to an estimated relevance signal. A lightweight relevance estimator is trained with teacher-model guidance and optimized to preserve the agent's decision consistency while reducing visual-token consumption. Experiments report over 50% token reduction without degrading task performance, and RAA is presented as a plug-in perception layer compatible with other memory methods.

**中文**

论文针对在长时程复杂任务中积累大量交互历史的LLM驱动智能体的高效记忆管理问题，提出感知粒度也是关键这一观点。作者提出“分辨率作为注意力”（RAA）：将过去交互渲染为图像，并根据估计的相关性动态调整每条交互的视觉分辨率。一个轻量的相关性估计器在教师模型指导下训练，并优化以在降低视觉令牌消耗的同时保持智能体决策一致性。实验报告在不降低任务性能的前提下可节省超过50%的令牌消耗，并将RAA描述为可与现有记忆方法结合的插件感知层。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works address agent memory efficiency and management for complex, long-horizon tasks. Differences: the paper focuses on adaptive perceptual compression of past interactions (visual-resolution allocation), whereas the thesis focuses on constructing and using persistent structured graph memory (知识图谱) built from extracted triples for multi-hop retrieval and QA. Limitations: RAA does not address triple extraction, entity resolution, graph coherence, or multi-hop path reuse. Complementarity: RAA could serve as a preprocessing or perception layer to reduce token/compute costs when rendering interaction traces for triple extraction or other downstream graph-building components.

**中文**

重合点：两者都关注复杂长时程任务中智能体记忆的效率与管理。差异：该论文侧重于对过去交互进行自适应感知压缩（以视觉分辨率分配为手段），而本论文则侧重于从交互中提取三元组并增量构建可持久化的结构化图记忆（知识图谱）以支持多跳检索与问答。局限性：RAA 未涉及三元组抽取、实体消歧、图结构一致性或多跳路径重用问题。互补性：RAA 可作为预处理/感知层，用以在将交互记录用于三元组抽取或图构建时降低令牌与计算开销。
