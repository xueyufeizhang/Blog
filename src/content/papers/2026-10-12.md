### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses automated safety interpretation of concrete-dam crack apertures when crack-gauge records are incomplete and abnormal changes are hard to distinguish from normal evolution. It proposes a pipeline combining a deep visual extractor (MDS-YOLOv11n) to derive crack widths from images, alignment with gauge measurements to fill missing observations, and Autoformer for fused-sequence prediction. A dynamic three-sigma criterion and a Heaviside–EMA reconstruction identify and reconstruct mutation-induced increments. Finally, GraphRAG links mutation-analysis outputs to domain safety knowledge to produce interpretable recommendations; validation on a dam case shows improved prediction versus comparative models and GraphRAG recommendations consistent with inspection records.

**中文**

文章解决了在裂缝尺记录不完整且异常变化难以区分于正常演化时，混凝土坝裂缝开度的自动安全解读问题。提出了一个流水线：使用深度视觉提取器 MDS-YOLOv11n 从图像中提取裂缝宽度，和裂缝尺测量对齐以补全缺失观测，使用 Autoformer 对融合序列建模预测。通过动态三西格玛准则识别持久偏离，并用 Heaviside–EMA 重建变异引起的增量。最后，GraphRAG 将变异分析结果与坝体裂缝安全知识相连接以产生可解释的建议；在一个混凝土坝案例上的验证显示相比对比模型有更好的预测性能，且 GraphRAG 的建议与定期检查记录一致。
