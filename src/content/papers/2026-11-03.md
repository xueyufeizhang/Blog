**English**

Worth a skim: the paper provides a substantial multimodal benchmark and a modular diagnostic methodology that can inform evaluation design and ablation studies for graph-memory RAG systems, but it does not directly study persistent KG-based memory or entity resolution.

**中文**

建议略读：该文给出了实质性的多模态基准与模块化诊断方法，可为图记忆型 RAG 系统的评估设计和消融实验提供借鉴，但并不直接研究持久化的基于 KG 的记忆或实体消解。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper introduces DamageRAG, a benchmark and diagnostic pipeline for multi-hop retrieval-augmented reasoning in structural damage inspection. The authors release dacl10k v2 (5,794 multiple-choice questions over 7,910 images, 11 task types, 3 hop levels) and a modular pipeline that retrieves MLLM-generated structured Damage Reports by field matching and routes quantitative queries to programmatic solvers; no stage is trained. Using ground-truth Reports, a 7B model’s accuracy rises from 57.9% to 82.9%. A three-layer diagnosis separates retrieval, reasoning, and perception failures and finds perception to be the dominant bottleneck; LoRA fine-tuning of the perception model substantially improves perception metrics and overall pipeline accuracy. The paper also quantifies routing and query-structure recovery failure modes.

**中文**

本文提出 DamageRAG——一个面向结构损伤检测的多跳检索增强推理的基准与诊断流水线。作者发布了 dacl10k v2（5,794 道多项选择题、7,910 张图像、11 种任务类型、3 个跳级）以及一个模块化流水线：通过字段匹配检索由 MLLM 生成的结构化 Damage Reports，并将定量查询交给程序化求解器；各阶段均不训练。在使用人工标注的 Reports 时，7B 模型的准确率从 57.9% 提升至 82.9%。论文提出三层诊断以区分检索、推理与感知故障，结论是感知为主要瓶颈；对感知模型做 LoRA 微调能明显提升感知指标和整体准确率。文中还量化了路由和查询结构恢复的失效模式。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works study multi-hop retrieval and RAG-style pipelines and emphasize modular diagnosis of failure modes. Differences: DamageRAG focuses on multimodal structural damage inspection and diagnoses a perception bottleneck using MLLM-generated structured reports; it does not study persistent, incrementally constructed knowledge graphs, entity resolution, or agentic graph memory. Complementarity: the paper’s benchmark design and three-layer failure attribution can inform evaluation protocols and ablation studies for graph memory systems, for example by isolating perception- or retrieval-induced fragmentation of graph construction.

**中文**

重合点：两者都研究多跳检索与 RAG 流水线，并重视对故障模式的模块化诊断。差异：DamageRAG 聚焦多模态的结构损伤检测，诊断出感知瓶颈并使用 MLLM 生成的结构化报告；它不涉及持久的、增量构建的知识图谱、实体消解或智能体的图记忆。互补性：该文的基准设计和三层故障归因方法可为图记忆系统的评估与消融实验提供参考，例如用于分离感知或检索引起的图构建碎片化效应。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Emphasize modular failure attribution when positioning related work: separating retrieval, reasoning, and perception failures clarifies which component a proposed graph memory targets. Report oracle or ground-truth ablations (as in DamageRAG) to bound attainable gains from structural memory versus perception improvements.

**中文**

在相关工作撰写中强调模块化故障归因：区分检索、推理与感知故障有助于明确拟议的图记忆解决了哪个环节的问题。参考 DamageRAG 做法报告带有人工标注的上界消融，以界定结构化记忆相较于感知改进的潜在收益。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt a layered diagnostic evaluation similar to DamageRAG: include oracle/ground-truth Report or retrieval ablations to measure upper bounds on downstream QA and to distinguish failures that arise before graph insertion. Route quantitative subproblems to deterministic solvers where appropriate to reduce confounding by model generation. Measure how perception errors propagate to graph construction (e.g., node/edge extraction error rates) and to multi-hop retrieval performance.

**中文**

采用类似 DamageRAG 的分层诊断评估：加入人工标注的 Report 或检索上界消融，以衡量下游 QA 的上限并区分图插入前产生的故障。对可量化的子问题使用确定性求解器以降低生成模型引入的混淆。衡量感知误差如何传导到图构建（例如节点/边抽取错误率）以及对多跳检索性能的影响。

### Future directions

**English**

Evaluate whether persistent graph memory reduces the perception-driven failure modes identified here, e.g., by using accumulated entities and relations to stabilize detection-to-entity mapping. Extend the benchmark to include graph-structured ground truth or bridge-entity annotations to test multi-hop traversal over an explicit KG constructed incrementally.

**中文**

评估持久化图记忆能否缓解本文识别的感知驱动故障，例如利用累积的实体与关系稳定检测到实体的映射。将基准扩展为包含图结构化的标注或桥接实体注释，以测试基于增量构建的显式知识图谱的多跳路径遍历。

## 02 · [Development and evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and GraphRAG for guideline-based clinical decision support in chronic kidney disease](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjdh-2026-000041)

高度相关：该论文直接评估了 GraphRAG，记录了具有临床意义的权衡（患者特异性 vs 清晰度）和评估实践，对本论文有启发意义。尽管规模小且领域特定，其方法学与失败模式对于设计增量图记忆实验及展示/验证标准具有参考价值。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This study compares a baseline LLM (GPT-4o), a vector-indexed RAG pipeline, and a knowledge-graph-enhanced GraphRAG pipeline for guideline-based clinical decision support in chronic kidney disease (CKD). Each approach answered nine clinical questions for a synthetic cohort of 70 CKD patients; outputs were judged by clinicians and via an LLM-as-Judge framework. RAG methods outperformed the baseline LLM on clinical correctness and guideline adherence. GraphRAG achieved the highest patient-specificity by leveraging multi-hop relationships from a knowledge graph derived from NICE CKD guidelines, but it scored lower in clarity because graph walks returned long guideline excerpts. All systems suffered when necessary guideline content was absent.

**中文**

该研究比较了基线 LLM（GPT-4o）、基于向量索引的检索增强生成（RAG）流水线与基于知识图谱的 GraphRAG 流水线，在慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）指南驱动的临床决策支持情境中表现如何。每种方法针对一个包含 70 名合成 CKD 患者的队列回答 9 个临床问题；输出由临床医生评估并采用 LLM-as-Judge 框架辅助判定。RAG 方法在临床正确性和指南一致性上优于基线 LLM。GraphRAG 通过利用从 NICE 指南导出的知识图谱中的多跳关系实现了最高的患者特异性，但因图路径返回了较长的指南摘录而在清晰度上得分较低。所有系统在关键指南内容缺失时表现不佳。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both this paper and the thesis evaluate GraphRAG-style pipelines and examine how knowledge-graph structure supports multi-hop, guideline-linked reasoning. Differences: the paper uses a domain-specific knowledge graph derived from NICE guidelines and a small, synthetic cohort (9 questions × 70 patients), whereas the thesis emphasizes an incrementally assembled graph memory created from the system's own interactions and evaluates on a larger multi-hop retrieval benchmark. Complementarity: the paper's clinical use case and clinician-in-the-loop evaluation illuminate practical trade-offs—particularly specificity versus clarity—that the thesis can investigate in a more general, incremental graph-memory setting.

**中文**

重合点：该论文与论文主题都评估了类似 GraphRAG 的流水线，考察知识图谱结构如何支持多跳、与指南相关的推理。差异：该论文使用从 NICE 指南导出的领域特定知识图谱并基于小规模合成队列（9 个问题 × 70 名患者）进行评估，而本硕士论文侧重于由系统自身交互增量构建的图记忆，并在更大规模的多跳检索基准上进行比较。互补性：该论文的临床用例与临床医生参与的评估揭示了实用的权衡（尤其是特异性与清晰度之间），这些都是本论文在通用增量图记忆场景中值得进一步探究的问题。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Report graph construction provenance and presentation format explicitly: how guideline text was transformed into KG triples and how graph walks were surfaced materially affect clarity and verifiability. Include clinician-facing metrics (patient-specificity, clarity) alongside standard retrieval/QA accuracy to capture practical utility.

**中文**

明确报告图构建的来源与呈现格式：指南文本如何被转为知识三元组以及图路径如何被展示，会显著影响清晰度和可验证性。应在标准检索/问答准确性之外，同时包含面向临床人员的指标（患者特异性、清晰度）来衡量实用价值。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Compare pre-built, guideline-derived KGs with incrementally assembled graph memory under identical tasks to measure benefits of persistent, evolving graph memory. Quantify clarity and audibility of graph-walk outputs (e.g., excerpt length, provenance links) and log failure modes when indexed guideline scope is insufficient. Use both human clinician judgments and LLM-as-judge but report inter-rater agreement and cases of disagreement.

**中文**

在相同任务下比较预构建的、基于指南的知识图谱与增量组装的图记忆，以量化持久、演化图记忆的优势。对图路径输出的清晰度与可审计性进行量化（例如摘录长度、来源链接），并记录当索引指南范围不足时的失败模式。采用临床医生评审与 LLM-as-Judge 的组合，但报告评审者间一致性及分歧案例。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend evaluation to larger multi-hop benchmarks and to scenarios where the graph is incrementally constructed from agent interactions; investigate methods to summarize or abstract long guideline excerpts to improve clarity without losing provenance. Explore hybrid entity-resolution and confidence-weighting strategies to maintain graph coherence in a growing, noisy memory.

**中文**

将评估扩展到更大规模的多跳基准，并针对由智能体交互增量构建图的情形进行测试；研究在不丢失来源信息的前提下对长指南摘录进行摘要或抽象以提高清晰度。探索混合的实体解析与置信度加权策略，以在不断增长且有噪声的记忆中维持图的一致性。
