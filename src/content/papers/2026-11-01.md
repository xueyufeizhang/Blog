### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper presents a controlled, reproducible benchmarking framework that compares three retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures—GraphRAG, LightRAG, and Linear RAG—on common datasets, embedding models, chunking strategies, prompts, and evaluation metrics. Experiments on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue measure retrieval effectiveness, answer quality, robustness to reasoning depth, and computational efficiency. GraphRAG attains the best multi-hop evidence recall and answer quality (with reported metrics such as Recall@10, nDCG@10, Evidence F1, Complete Evidence Retrieval, and Answer F1), and shows greater robustness on four-hop questions, at the expense of higher latency and resource use. The framework is presented as reusable for transparent, deployment-friendly assessment of RAG variants.

**中文**

本文提出了一个受控且可复现的基准评估框架，比对三种检索增强生成（RAG）架构：GraphRAG、LightRAG 和 Linear RAG，在统一的数据集、嵌入模型、切片策略、提示模板和评估方法下进行比较。对 HotpotQA、2WikiMultiHopQA 与 MuSiQue 的实验评估了检索有效性、答案质量、对推理深度的鲁棒性以及计算效率。GraphRAG 在多跳证据召回和答案质量上表现最好（使用 Recall@10、nDCG@10、Evidence F1、Complete Evidence Retrieval、Answer F1 等指标），且在四跳问题上更稳健，但代价是更高的延迟和资源消耗。作者将该框架作为一个可复用的、便于部署的评估工具。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: The paper directly evaluates graph-structured retrieval (GraphRAG) versus linear/light RAG on multi-hop QA, providing metrics and datasets that match the thesis's multi-hop retrieval focus. Differences/limitations: It appears to target static retrieval architectures and does not address agentic RAG with persistent, incrementally constructed graph memory, nor the entity-resolution and incremental graph-coherence issues central to the thesis. Complementarity: Its reproducible benchmark, metrics, and reported trade-offs between effectiveness and resource costs can serve as a baseline and experimental protocol for comparing the thesis's agentic, persistent graph-memory system against non-agentic GraphRAG variants.

**中文**

重合点：该论文直接比较了图结构检索（GraphRAG）与线性/轻量 RAG 在多跳问答上的表现，提供了与论文多跳检索目标相匹配的指标和数据集。差异/局限：该工作聚焦于静态检索架构，未涉及具有持久化、增量构建图记忆的智能体式 RAG，也未处理论文强调的实体消歧与增量图一致性问题。互补性：其可复现的基准、评估指标与有关效果—资源成本之间权衡的结论，可作为比较论文所提智能体化持久图记忆系统与非智能体 GraphRAG 变体的基线和实验协议。

**English**

When positioning the thesis, cite this paper for benchmark design, metrics, and empirical trade-offs between graph-structured and lighter retrievals. Emphasize the novelty of adding agentic persistent graph memory, incremental triple extraction, and explicit entity-resolution strategies not covered by this benchmark.

**中文**

在论文定位中引用该工作以说明基准设计、评估指标及图结构检索与轻量检索之间的经验权衡。强调本论文在引入智能体持久化图记忆、增量三元组抽取与明确的实体消歧策略方面的创新，这些是该基准未覆盖的。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt the paper's controlled benchmark practices: standardize datasets (e.g., HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue), embedding models, chunking/prompt configurations, and metrics (Recall@10, nDCG@10, Evidence F1, Complete Evidence Retrieval, Answer F1) for fair comparisons. Extend their protocol by running experiments that replace static GraphRAG retrieval with your incrementally assembled graph memory and by measuring bridge-entity identification, evolving graph coherence, and retrieval latency as memory accumulates over interactions.

**中文**

借用论文的受控基准做法：统一使用标准数据集（如 HotpotQA、2WikiMultiHopQA、MuSiQue）、嵌入模型、切片/提示配置及指标（Recall@10、nDCG@10、Evidence F1、Complete Evidence Retrieval、Answer F1）以确保公平比较。在此协议上扩展实验，将静态 GraphRAG 检索替换为增量组装的图记忆，并测量桥接实体识别、随记忆累积变化的图一致性以及检索延迟等指标。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate the benchmark into experiments that test agentic RAG with persistent, incrementally constructed graph memory; add controlled comparisons of entity-resolution strategies (embedding-similarity vs. LLM-as-judge) and measure performance evolution across repeated interactions. Also evaluate resource trade-offs on consumer-grade local deployments to match the thesis's practical setup.

**中文**

将该基准用于测试具有持久化、增量构建图记忆的智能体式 RAG；加入对实体消歧策略（嵌入相似度 vs. LLM 评判器）的受控比较，并测量随多次交互性能的演进。还应在消费级本地部署上评估资源权衡，以匹配论文的实践场景。

### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper surveys Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) design choices for private document question answering and describes an implemented multi-PDF RAG pipeline. The system extracts text with PyMuPDF (OCR fallback), builds dense embeddings with BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5, stores vectors in ChromaDB, and retrieves semantically similar chunks by cosine distance. Answers are generated by a local Llama 3.2:3B model served via Ollama; the implementation uses React/Vite and FastAPI for document management and APIs. The survey reviews hybrid retrieval, reranking, and GraphRAG variants and discusses strengths, limitations, and future directions for consumer-grade local architectures.

**中文**

本文综述了面向私有文档问答的检索增强生成（RAG）设计选项，并介绍了一个已实现的多PDF RAG流水线。系统使用 PyMuPDF（含 OCR 回退）抽取文本，采用 BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 构建稠密嵌入，向量存储在 ChromaDB 中，基于余弦距离检索语义相似文本块。答案由本地 Llama 3.2:3B（通过 Ollama 提供服务）生成；实现基于 React/Vite 和 FastAPI 提供文档管理与查询接口。综述评估了混合检索、重排序和 GraphRAG 等方法，并讨论了面向消费者级本地架构的优劣与未来方向。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both address RAG for private/local document QA and consider consumer-grade deployment decisions. Differences: this paper is an engineering-focused survey and multi-PDF pipeline emphasizing vector retrieval (ChromaDB \+ bge) and local LLM hosting, whereas the thesis focuses on incremental structured graph memory, agentic RAG, entity resolution, and multi-hop reasoning with persistent graph assembly. Complementarity: the survey provides practical baseline design choices and engineering components useful for implementing the thesis's vector baselines and deployment, but it does not evaluate or propose persistent graph memory or agentic orchestration.

**中文**

重叠点：两者都涉及面向私有/本地文档问答的 RAG，以及面向消费者级部署的工程抉择。差异：该论文是面向工程实现的综述并介绍了以向量检索（ChromaDB \+ bge）为核心的多PDF流水线，而本论文主题聚焦于增量构建的结构化图记忆、智能体驱动的 RAG、实体解析与持久化图组装及多跳推理。互补性：该综述为实现论文中的向量基线和部署细节提供了实用组件及设计建议，但未涉及持久化图记忆或智能体编排的评估或方法。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper as an engineering-systems reference when describing document ingestion, embedding choices, vector storage, and local LLM hosting. Cite it for practical trade-offs about consumer-grade architectures, but do not rely on it for methods related to persistent graph memory, entity resolution, or agentic reasoning.

**中文**

在讨论文档抽取、嵌入选择、向量存储与本地 LLM 托管时，可将该论文作为工程系统参考。引用它以说明面向消费者级架构的实用权衡，但不要依赖其作为持久化图记忆、实体解析或智能体推理方法的来源。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Use the paper's pipeline components (PyMuPDF, bge-small, ChromaDB, Ollama-hosted local Llama) as a reproducible vector-RAG baseline for experiments comparing to graph memory. Re-evaluate retrieval settings (chunking, embedding model, DB) on MultiHop-RAG and measure bridge-entity recall, end-to-end latency, and retrieval precision to isolate gains from structured graph memory. Report resource and latency numbers for fair comparison on consumer-grade hardware.

**中文**

可将该论文的流水线组件（PyMuPDF、bge-small、ChromaDB、通过 Ollama 托管的本地 Llama）作为可复现的向量RAG基线，用于与图记忆方法比较。在 MultiHop-RAG 上重新调优检索设置（分块、嵌入模型、数据库）并衡量桥接实体召回、端到端延迟和检索精度，以量化结构化图记忆带来的增益。为在消费者级硬件上进行公平比较，应报告资源消耗和延迟数据。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate GraphRAG or an explicit incremental graph layer into the described multi-PDF pipeline to compare vector-only versus query-aware graph memory for multi-hop QA. Extend the system to extract triples and track provenance, and evaluate entity-resolution strategies and agentic orchestration on the same hardware-constrained stack.

**中文**

将 GraphRAG 或显式的增量图层集成到所述多PDF流水线中，以比较纯向量与查询感知图记忆在多跳问答上的效果。扩展系统以提取三元组并记录溯源，评估实体解析策略与智能体编排在相同受限硬件栈上的表现。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper surveys lifelong-memory architectures that provide persistent external memory for LLM-based generative systems, addressing the statelessness that causes loss of information across sessions. It traces developments from early external-memory augmentations and reflective generative agents to memory operating systems, graph-shaped memory, and reinforcement learning approaches for agent memory. The authors propose an operation-centered classification and compare twelve memory representations on dimensions such as scalability, retrieval efficiency, personalization, computational cost, and adaptability. They also discuss enabling technologies including embeddings, approximate nearest neighbor indexes, knowledge graphs, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

**中文**

本文综述了为基于大型语言模型的生成式系统提供持久外部记忆的终身记忆架构，旨在解决会话外信息丢失的问题。文章回顾了从早期外部记忆增强和反思式生成智能体，到记忆操作系统、图状记忆和基于强化学习的智能体记忆的发展。作者提出了一种以操作为中心的分类，并在可扩展性、检索效率、个性化、计算成本和适应性等维度上比较了十二种记忆表示，同时讨论了嵌入、近似最近邻索引、知识图谱（KG）和检索增强生成（RAG）等支撑技术。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Directly relevant: the survey covers graph-shaped memory and operational trade-offs (scalability, retrieval efficiency, cost) that map to the thesis's interest in persistent structured graph memory. It is limited in that it is a high-level comparison rather than an empirical evaluation of an implemented, incremental KG assembled from agent interactions. The paper complements the thesis by supplying a taxonomy and evaluation axes that can be applied when designing experiments (e.g., latency, consolidation, forgetting), but it likely lacks specific methods for entity resolution and multi-hop retrieval benchmarks used in the thesis.

**中文**

直接相关：该综述涵盖了图状记忆及其在可扩展性、检索效率和成本等操作性权衡，这些与论文关注的持久结构化图记忆相契合。其局限在于比较偏概念性，而非对从智能体交互中增量构建的知识图谱（图记忆）进行实证评估。该论文可补充论文的工作，通过提供分类法和可用于实验设计的评估维度（例如延迟、巩固、遗忘），但可能不包含论文所需的实体解析方法和多跳检索基准的具体实现细节。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this survey as the authoritative source for the landscape and operational taxonomy of lifelong-memory systems. Use its classification to position the thesis's contribution (incremental KG assembled from agent activity and empirical comparison with vector RAG and agentic RAG without long-term memory). Note where the survey stops short of implementation-level guidance.

**中文**

将此综述作为终身记忆系统全景与操作性分类的权威引用。用其分类来定位论文贡献（由智能体活动增量构建的知识图谱以及与纯向量RAG和无持久记忆的智能体RAG的实证比较）。指出该综述在实现层面缺乏指导的地方。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt the paper's evaluation axes (scalability, retrieval efficiency, personalization, computational cost, adaptability) as explicit metrics in experiments. Implement comparisons across memory representations suggested by the survey: e.g., embedding/ANN-based retrieval vs graph-shaped memory, measuring both accuracy (multi-hop QA, bridge-entity ID) and operational metrics (end-to-end latency, memory update cost). Include lifecycle behaviors the survey highlights—writing, consolidation, forgetting—when designing memory-update protocols.

**中文**

在实验中采用该论文提出的评估维度（可扩展性、检索效率、个性化、计算成本、适应性）作为显式指标。实现综述中建议的记忆表示比较，例如嵌入/ANN检索与图状记忆的对比，同时测量准确性（多跳问答、桥接实体识别）和运行指标（端到端延迟、记忆更新成本）。在设计记忆更新协议时纳入综述强调的生命周期行为——写入、整合、遗忘。

**English**

Empirically extend the survey by benchmarking the surveyed memory architectures under controlled conditions using the same LLM(s) and datasets (e.g., multi-hop QA). Investigate hybrid approaches that combine vector indexes with query-aware graph memory, and evaluate concrete entity-resolution strategies and their effect on graph coherence and multi-hop retrieval.

**中文**
