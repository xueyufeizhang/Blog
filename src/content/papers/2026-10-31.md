### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper presents a provenance-aware framework for extracting and reconciling entity and relation mentions from text, images, and audio-visual dialogue. It pipelines established OCR, object detection, ASR, speaker diarization, and transformer-based language modules to produce a unified mention schema, aligns mentions across modalities using temporal, semantic, and contextual signals, and applies a confidence-aware fusion that merges corroborating mentions while flagging conflicts. Reconciled facts are persisted in a knowledge graph whose edges record source modality, source segment, and fused confidence. A hybrid retrieval workflow combining dense vector search with graph traversal is proposed; the authors describe system architecture and an evaluation protocol for a prototype.

**中文**

本文提出了一个可溯源的框架，用于从文本、图像与视听对话中抽取并调和实体和关系提及。该框架串联现有的 OCR、目标检测、ASR、说话人分离和基于 Transformer 的语言模块，生成统一的提及模式，通过时间、语义和上下文证据对跨模态提及进行对齐，并采用带置信度的融合步骤在合并互为佐证的提及的同时显式标记冲突。调和后的事实被持久化为知识图谱（KG），其边携带来源模态、来源片段和融合置信度。文中还提出了将稠密向量检索与图遍历相结合的混合检索流程，并描述了原型系统的架构与评估协议。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses limitations of flat vector retrieval for multi-hop question answering over enterprise corpora by proposing BeamRAG v2, a second-generation GraphRAG retrieval engine. Contributions are: (1) pre-traversal document-scoped alias-cluster mapping that groups alias forms under canonical representatives for seed deduplication and loop pruning; (2) query-complexity-adaptive beam width B(q) ∈ \[4,8\] that scales breadth by syntactic clause density; (3) thresholded continuous power-law hub regularization with leaf dampening to suppress high-degree hubs while leaving low-degree nodes intact; and (4) fine-grained edge-to-chunk syntactic provenance tracking with macro-coherence-gated cross-document bridge traversal. Evaluation on a 500-query, four-domain enterprise benchmark shows statistically significant gains over BFS and a LightRAG deployment, and mixed results versus BeamRAG v1 across two tracks.

**中文**

本文针对平坦向量检索在企业语料多跳问答中的局限，提出了第二代 GraphRAG 检索引擎 BeamRAG v2。主要贡献包括： (1) 预遍历的文档范围别名簇映射，将多种别名形式归并为规范代表以实现种子去重和路径循环剪枝；(2) 基于查询复杂度的自适应束宽 B(q) ∈ \[4,8\]，按句法从句密度调整搜索宽度；(3) 带叶子抑制的阈值化连续幂律枢纽正则化，在平滑抑制高连通枢纽的同时保持低度节点不受影响；(4) 细粒度的边到文本块句法溯源追踪，并结合宏一致性门控进行跨文档桥接遍历。作者在一个覆盖四个领域的 500 条查询企业基准上进行评价，结果在统计上优于 BFS 和 LightRAG 部署，对 BeamRAG v1 在两个轨道上表现不一。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both this paper and the thesis focus on knowledge-graph-augmented retrieval for multi-hop QA and address alias/alias-clustering and hub effects in graph traversal. Differences: BeamRAG v2 concentrates on traversal algorithms, alias-cluster indexing, hub regularization, and provenance for a static enterprise graph, whereas the thesis studies agentic RAG with an incrementally assembled persistent graph memory and contrasts embedding vs LLM-based entity-resolution strategies. Complementarity: BeamRAG v2 techniques (alias-clustering, adaptive beam width, hub regularization, provenance tracking) are directly applicable components for improving traversal and verifiability in an agentic graph memory system.

**中文**

重合点：论文与毕业设计均关注基于知识图谱（KG）的多跳检索，并解决别名聚类与枢纽节点在图遍历中的影响。差异：BeamRAG v2 侧重于遍历算法、别名簇索引、枢纽正则化与溯源能力，基于静态企业图进行评估；而论文课题聚焦于由智能体驱动的检索增强生成（RAG）和渐增构建的持久化图记忆，并比较嵌入相似度与 LLM 评判的实体消歧策略。互补性：BeamRAG v2 的别名聚类、自适应束宽、枢纽正则化与溯源追踪可作为改进查询感知图记忆遍历与可验证性的模块。

### Research synopsis

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses retrieval for tax-risk analysis, where evidence sets must include governing provisions, companion rules, and an explicit relation path rather than isolated passages. It proposes Typed Evidence-Budgeted GraphRAG (TEB-GraphRAG), a GraphRAG variant that jointly scores lexical seeds, relation types, and uses a deterministic two-topic sparse gate while preserving graph-path provenance. The authors construct a transparent synthetic collection (59 tax-section identifiers, 238 directed relations, 700 templated issues) and evaluate 500 held-out queries without training a learned retriever. TEB-GraphRAG achieves 85.40% Recall@5, outperforming unrestricted one-hop graph expansion (69.27%) and BM25 (59.93%), and shows strong sensitivity to type-label fidelity and topic distractors under stress tests.

**中文**

本文针对税务风险分析中的检索问题，其中所需证据集应包含主管条款、配套规则及明确的关系路径，而不是孤立片段。作者提出了 Typed Evidence-Budgeted GraphRAG（TEB-GraphRAG），一种 GraphRAG 变体，联合评分词汇种子、关系类型，并使用确定性稀疏的双主题门控，同时保留图路径溯源。论文构建了一个透明的合成集合（59 个税节标识符、238 条有向关系、700 条模板化问题），在 500 条保留查询上无监督评估。TEB-GraphRAG 达到 85.40% 的 Recall@5，优于不受限一跳图扩展（69.27%）和 BM25（59.93%），并在类型标签一致性和话题干扰的压力测试中表现出高度敏感性。
