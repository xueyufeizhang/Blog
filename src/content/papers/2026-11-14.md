### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper studies integrity threats to Knowledge Graph RAG (KG-RAG) systems by formally defining and measuring the "blast radius"—the number of corrupted queries caused per injected triple—under three triple-level poisoning strategies: edge poisoning, hub poisoning, and chain poisoning. Using a biomedical knowledge graph built with Microsoft GraphRAG v0.3.6 and Gemma 2 9B, the authors report per-triple blast radii of 14.0× for hub poisoning, 7.0× for edge poisoning, and 4.0× for chain poisoning from a 0% baseline. Two-hop queries exhibit the steadiest contamination, and cascade effects exceed direct impact. They propose a degree-weighted trust filter that uses entity centrality as an injection-risk proxy; at the reported operating point it queues the hub injections and 4.1% of baseline triples while not queuing edge or chain injections.

**中文**

本文研究知识图谱（KG）RAG 系统的完整性威胁，形式化并量化所谓的“冲击半径”——每注入一条三元组导致被污染查询的数量——针对三种三元组级投毒策略：边投毒（edge poisoning）、枢纽投毒（hub poisoning）和链投毒（chain poisoning）。在使用 Microsoft GraphRAG v0.3.6 和 Gemma 2 9B 构建的生物医学知识图谱上，作者报告每条三元组的冲击半径分别为枢纽投毒 14.0×、边投毒 7.0×、链投毒 4.0×（以 0% 预投毒基线计）。两跳查询受污染最稳定，级联效应在所有条件下均超出直接影响。作者提出基于度数加权信任分的过滤器，使用实体中心性作为注入风险代理；在所报告的工作点，该过滤器将枢纽注入和 4.1% 的基线三元组加入队列，但不会对边或链注入排队。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Directly relevant to the thesis' concern with graph memory robustness: the paper characterizes how single corrupted triples can propagate through typed-edges multi-hop inference, which threatens incremental graph memory assembled by agentic RAG. It complements the thesis by providing a concrete threat model and a measurable blast-radius metric, plus a simple centrality-based defense. Limitations relative to the thesis: empirical work is on a preconstructed biomedical KG and does not address incrementally constructed graph memory, LLM extraction noise, or the paper's specific agentic paradigms and entity-resolution strategies that the thesis evaluates.

**中文**

与论文关于图记忆鲁棒性的关注直接相关：该论文刻画了单条被篡改三元组如何通过有类型边的多跳推理传播，这会威胁由智能体驱动的增量图记忆。它通过可度量的冲击半径指标和一个中心性驱动的防御方法补充了本论文的工作。与本论文相比的局限性在于：其实证工作基于预构建的生物医学 KG，并未处理增量构建的图记忆、LLM 提取噪声，亦未涉及本论文评估的具体智能体范式和实体解析策略。

**English**

Position this paper in related work as a threat-model and robustness study for structured graph memory. Cite its blast-radius formalism when motivating evaluation metrics for graph coherence and contamination. Note that its defense is lightweight and heuristic (centrality proxy) rather than LLM-aware.

**中文**

将本文放在相关工作中作为结构化图记忆的威胁建模与鲁棒性研究。在论述图一致性与污染评估指标时引用其冲击半径形式化。注意其所提防御为轻量启发式（基于中心性代理），而非基于 LLM 的检测方法。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt the paper's blast-radius metric to quantify how injected or extracted erroneous triples affect multi-hop retrieval and QA accuracy in your incremental graph memory. Re-run poisoning experiments in an agentic, incrementally assembled graph setting (not just a prebuilt KG) to measure cascade vs direct effects when triples are produced by LLM extractors. Evaluate the degree-weighted trust filter's false positive/negative trade-offs across different entity-resolution strategies and agentic paradigms.

**中文**

采用该文的冲击半径指标来量化注入或提取错误三元组对多跳检索和问答准确率的影响。将投毒实验迁移到智能体增量构建的图记忆环境中（而非仅在预构建 KG 上），以测量当三元组由 LLM 提取器产生时级联效应与直接效应的差异。评估度数加权信任过滤器在不同实体解析策略和智能体范式下的假阳性/假阴性权衡。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend the evaluation to triples produced by LLM-based extractors within agentic interactions to measure realistic noise and adversarial overlap. Combine centrality-based heuristics with provenance, confidence scores, and entity-resolution signals to improve detection. Test defenses under incremental graph growth and reuse scenarios the thesis targets.

**中文**

将评估扩展到由智能体交互中基于 LLM 的提取器产生的三元组，以衡量真实噪声和对抗重叠。将基于中心性的启发式方法与来源信息、置信度分和实体解析信号结合以改进检测。针对本论文关注的增量图增长与重用场景测试防御效果。

**English**

The paper provides concrete empirical evidence for structured KG retrieval (neo4j/Cypher) improving answer quality and a detailed hallucination taxonomy; both are directly useful for designing baselines, evaluation metrics, and error analyses in the thesis despite its domain restriction.

**中文**

论文提供了结构化知识图谱检索（neo4j/Cypher）改善答案质量的实证证据及详尽的幻觉分类；尽管受域限制，这些内容对于论文中基线设计、评估指标和误差分析具有直接参考价值。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses hallucination in legal academic viewpoint question answering by building a GraphRAG framework over a corpus of Chinese core legal journals. It compares two retrieval strategies (Vector and Cypher) across three LLMs and reports that using Cypher retrieval within a neo4j\_graphrag setup reduced gpt-4-turbo's unanswerable rate to 3.41% and achieved a BERTScore-F1\_avg of 80.58%. The authors classify five GraphRAG-specific hallucination types and argue that structured knowledge-graph retrieval mitigates traditional knowledge-deficiency hallucinations but redistributes hallucination sources toward the joint retrieval-and-generation process. The study is scoped to a closed corpus on “Legal Regulation of AI Training Data.”

**中文**

本文针对法律学术观点问答中的幻觉问题，在中国核心法律期刊语料上构建 GraphRAG 框架，比较了 Vector 与 Cypher 两种检索策略在三种 LLM 上的表现。结果表明，在 neo4j\_graphrag 架构下，使用 Cypher 检索使 gpt-4-turbo 的不可回答率降至 3.41%，BERTScore-F1\_avg 达到 80.58%。作者归纳了五类 GraphRAG 特有的幻觉类型，指出结构化的知识图谱检索能缓解传统的知识匮乏型幻觉，但会将幻觉来源转移到检索与生成的协同过程中。研究限定于“AI 训练数据的法律规制”这一封闭语料域。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both investigate structured KG-backed retrieval (GraphRAG) and the impact of graph-based retrieval on answer quality and hallucination. Differences: this paper is domain-specific (legal, closed corpus) and studies a prebuilt KG retrieval pipeline (neo4j\_graphrag) rather than an incrementally assembled graph memory created from agent interactions; it does not address entity-resolution strategies or compare agentic paradigms and local-model deployments. Complementarity: the paper's empirical comparison of Vector vs Cypher retrieval and its hallucination taxonomy can inform evaluation metrics and error analysis in the thesis.

**中文**

重合点：两者都研究基于知识图谱（KG）的结构化检索（GraphRAG）及其对答案质量和幻觉的影响。差异：该论文限于法律领域的封闭语料，并采用基于 neo4j 的图检索管线，而非由智能体交互增量构建的图记忆；论文未处理实体解析策略，也未比较不同的智能体范式或在本地模型上的部署。互补性：其对 Vector 与 Cypher 检索的实证比较与幻觉分类可为论文的评估指标与误差分析提供参考。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper as a domain-focused case study showing that graph-backed Cypher retrieval can improve answerability and reduce certain hallucinations. Use its hallucination taxonomy and reported metrics (unanswerable rate, BERTScore-F1\_avg) when situating your work, but emphasize differences in corpus scope and the absence of incremental graph memory and entity-resolution experiments.

**中文**

将该论文定位为域内案例研究，表明基于图的 Cypher 检索可提高可答率并减少部分幻觉。在撰写相关工作时可引用其幻觉分类和指标（不可回答率、BERTScore-F1\_avg），但需强调语料范围与增量图记忆及实体解析实验的缺失。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Adopt the paper's explicit comparison of Vector vs Cypher retrieval as a practical baseline: measure unanswerable rate and BERTScore-F1\_avg alongside QA accuracy. Reuse its hallucination taxonomy to annotate error types produced by structured retrieval in your multi-hop, agentic setting. Experimentally, integrate a neo4j-backed Cypher retrieval baseline against your incremental graph memory to quantify gains attributable to persistence and entity-resolution choices.

**中文**

可将论文中 Vector 与 Cypher 检索的显式比较作为实用基线：在 QA 准确率之外同时记录不可回答率与 BERTScore-F1\_avg。利用其幻觉分类对结构化检索在多跳智能体场景中产生的错误进行标注。实验上，在你的增量图记忆方案中并行集成 neo4j 支持的 Cypher 检索基线，以量化持久化与实体解析选择带来的改进。

### Future directions

**English**

Extend this work beyond a closed legal corpus by building the graph incrementally from agent interactions and evaluating entity-resolution strategies. Combine their Cypher-based retrieval with agentic RAG paradigms and local LLMs to measure latency, persistence effects, and whether hallucination distributions change when the KG is assembled online.

**中文**

可将此研究从封闭法律语料扩展为由智能体交互增量构建的图，并评估实体解析策略。将其基于 Cypher 的检索与智能体驱动的 RAG 范式及本地 LLM 结合，测量延迟、持久化效应，以及在线组装的知识图谱是否改变幻觉分布。

Recommended for skimming: the paper gives concrete evidence that incorporating a prerequisite KG into retrieval can improve the relevance of generated distractors and demonstrates a useful validation pipeline, but it is domain-specific and does not address the thesis’ core topics of incremental agentic graph memory, entity resolution, or multi-hop QA.

**中文**

建议略读：该文提供了将先修知识图谱纳入检索可提升生成干扰项相关性的实证，以及一个有用的验证流程，但属于垂直领域研究，未触及论文的核心问题——增量智能体图记忆、实体解析或多跳问答。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses automatic generation of multiple‑choice mathematics assessments by integrating a directed acyclic prerequisite knowledge graph with retrieval-augmented generation (GraphRAG). The architecture combines a DAG of prerequisite relationships, semantic embeddings, hybrid retrieval, LLM-based item and distractor generation, and independent mathematical validation using SymPy. Evaluation covered 154 questions and 462 distractors, reporting that 89% of generated questions passed initial symbolic validation and that GraphRAG yielded a higher proportion of distractors with identifiable prerequisite relationships than conventional RAG. The authors also report a small teacher evaluation on perceived quality. They note the comparison does not fully isolate graph effects or establish downstream learning gains.

**中文**

该论文提出将有向无环的先修知识图谱与检索增强生成（GraphRAG）结合，用于自动生成选择题型的数学测评。体系结构整合了先修关系的DAG、语义嵌入、混合检索、基于LLM的试题与干扰项生成，以及使用SymPy进行独立的数学验证。评估包含154道题目与462个干扰项，报告89%的题目通过了初步符号验证，且与传统RAG相比，GraphRAG生成的干扰项中更大比例能识别出先修关系。作者同时进行了小规模的教师质量评估，并指出比较未能完全隔离图结构的贡献，也未证明对学生学习的影响。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both exploit graph-structured retrieval (GraphRAG) to improve downstream generation/retrieval and report comparative experiments between graph-based and conventional RAG. Differences: this paper is domain-specific (mathematics assessment) and uses a preconstructed DAG of prerequisite concepts rather than an incrementally assembled graph memory from agent interactions; it does not address entity resolution, agentic paradigms, or persistent graph accumulation. Complementarity: the study’s hybrid retrieval design and independent symbolic validation (SymPy) provide practical methods for verifiability and for measuring how graph structure influences retrieved context.

**中文**

重合点：两者都利用图结构检索（GraphRAG）来改善下游生成/检索，并进行了图基与传统RAG的比较实验。差异：该论文针对数学测评的垂直领域，使用预构建的先修概念DAG，而非来自智能体交互的增量图记忆；未涉及实体解析、智能体范式或持久图记忆的累积问题。互补性：其混合检索设计与基于SymPy的独立符号验证为可验证性提供了可借鉴的实用方法，并能用于评估图结构对检索上下文的影响。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Cite this paper as empirical evidence that graph-structured retrieval can increase retrieval of conceptually relevant context (here, prerequisite–distractor correspondence). Note the authors’ caveat that retrieval differences may not isolate graph contributions; emphasize the need for controlled retrieval-context matching when drawing conclusions.

**中文**

可将此文作为实验证据，表明图结构检索能提升检索到的概念相关上下文（如先修–干扰项对应性）。引用时应指出作者的保留意见：检索结果差异未必能完全归因于图结构，写作中应强调在得出结论时需要对检索上下文进行严格对照。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adopting an independent symbolic or domain validator (analogous to SymPy) to improve answer verifiability and to separate generation errors from retrieval errors. When comparing GraphRAG vs vector RAG, reproduce their approach of controlling 'maximum context' but add ablations that fix retrieved passages while varying only graph access to isolate graph contributions. Record per-item provenance (which graph edges/nodes were used) and measure bridge-entity correspondence or relation alignment as quantitative graph-utility metrics.

**中文**

可考虑采用独立的符号或领域验证器（类似SymPy）来提高答案可验证性，并将生成错误与检索错误区分开来。在比较GraphRAG与向量RAG时，可参考其对“最大上下文”限制的控制，但应增加消融实验——固定检索到的段落，仅改变是否允许访问图结构，以便隔离图的影响。记录每个题目的溯源信息（使用了哪些图节点/边），并测量桥接实体对应性或关系对齐等定量图效用指标。

### Future directions

**English**

Apply GraphRAG-style retrieval to multi-hop QA benchmarks and to incrementally constructed graph memory to test whether prerequisite-like edges learned or accumulated through interactions improve multi-hop path finding. Experimentally compare low-latency embedding-based entity resolution vs LLM-judge strategies in this generation setting and measure end-to-end latency and provenance for verifiability.

**中文**

将GraphRAG式检索应用于多跳问答基准，并在增量构建的图记忆上测试通过交互学得或累积的先修类边是否能改进多跳路径检索。还可在该生成场景中对比低延迟的嵌入式实体解析与基于LLM的裁判策略，并测量端到端延迟与溯源以评估可验证性。
