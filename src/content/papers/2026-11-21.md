### Research synopsis

**English**

This paper studies robustness of GraphRAG-style retrieval-augmented multi-hop QA when user queries are noisy or reformulated. The authors first quantify how character-, word-, and sentence-level query perturbations degrade GraphRAG performance on HotpotQA and 2WikiMultiHopQA. To mitigate failures that arise early in the pipeline (before graph expansion), they propose RER-GRAG, an entity reranking module, and an outcome-supervised variant, Tr-RER-GRAG. Experiments report consistent gains over baseline GraphRAG under perturbations (e.g., substantial relative accuracy improvements averaged per perturbation type on both benchmarks) and analyze reductions in perturbation-induced failures and sensitivity.

**中文**

本文研究了在用户查询存在噪声或重述时，基于 GraphRAG 的检索增强多跳问答的鲁棒性。作者首先在 HotpotQA 与 2WikiMultiHopQA 上，量化了字符级、词级与句级查询扰动对 GraphRAG 性能的影响。为缓解在图扩展之前产生并会传导到下游模块的失败，提出了实体重排序模块 RER-GRAG 及其结果监督变体 Tr-RER-GRAG。实验显示在扰动查询下相较基线 GraphRAG 有一致改进（例如在两套基准上按扰动类型平均的相对准确率有显著提升），并分析了对扰动导致失败率与敏感性的缓解效果。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

High overlap: both this paper and the thesis target GraphRAG-style graph retrieval for multi-hop QA and focus on the role of entity selection in end-to-end performance. Differences: the paper studies query perturbation robustness and proposes an entity reranking mitigation, whereas the thesis centers on persistent, incrementally assembled graph memory constructed from the system's own interactions and compares embedding vs LLM-based entity resolution. Complementarity: the reranking methods and robustness analyses could be applied to the thesis's incremental graph assembly and to the empirical comparison of low-latency embedding strategies versus an LLM-as-judge for entity resolution.

**中文**

高度相关：本文与论文均关注基于 GraphRAG 的多跳检索问答，并强调实体选择对端到端性能的影响。不同点：本文关注查询扰动鲁棒性并提出实体重排序作为缓解措施，而论文侧重于由系统交互增量构建的持久图记忆，并比较低延迟嵌入与 LLM 评判的实体解析策略。互补性：本文的重排序方法与鲁棒性分析可以用于论文提出的增量图组装，以及用于评估嵌入策略与 LLM 评判在实体解析上的折衷。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper in related work on robustness and entity-resolution: it provides concrete evidence that early-stage entity selection errors propagate in GraphRAG and that targeted reranking can substantially recover accuracy under noisy queries. Cite it when discussing query perturbations, pipeline fragility, and mitigation strategies for graph memory systems.

**中文**

在相关工作中将该文定位于鲁棒性与实体解析方向：它提供证据表明早期的实体选择错误会在 GraphRAG 管道中传播，且针对性的重排序可在噪声查询下显著恢复准确率。在讨论查询扰动、管道脆弱性及图记忆系统的缓解策略时应引用此文。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Actionable ideas: adapt RER-GRAG / Tr-RER-GRAG to an incremental graph-memory pipeline by applying reranking at entity extraction or candidate resolution steps before committing triples to the graph. Evaluate robustness using the same perturbation suites (character/word/sentence) and measure downstream effects on bridge-entity identification, multi-hop retrieval accuracy, and graph coherence. Quantify latency/quality trade-offs when running rerankers locally on consumer-grade models (e.g., Qwen2.5-7B) and compare their effect against the thesis's embedding-similarity and LLM-as-judge entity-resolution strategies.

**中文**

可操作建议：将 RER-GRAG / Tr-RER-GRAG 适配到增量图记忆管道，在将三元组写入图之前，对实体抽取或候选解析步骤执行重排序。使用相同的扰动套件（字符/词/句级）评估鲁棒性，并测量对桥接实体识别、多跳检索准确率和图连贯性的下游影响。量化在消费级本地模型（例如 Qwen2.5-7B）上运行重排序器的延迟/质量权衡，并将其效果与论文中嵌入相似度与 LLM-as-judge 实体解析策略比较。

### Future directions

**English**

Follow-ups include integrating outcome-supervised reranking into the thesis's persistent graph-building loop so reranking decisions influence which entities/relations are persisted, and evaluating whether reranking reduces graph fragmentation from surface-form variants. Another direction is to test the methods' compatibility with local, privacy-preserving deployments and to measure their impact on memory growth and long-term retrieval efficiency.

**中文**

后续工作可将结果监督的重排序集成到论文提出的持久图构建循环，使重排序决策影响哪些实体/关系被持久化，并评估重排序是否减少由表面形式差异导致的图碎片化。另一方向是测试这些方法在本地、隐私保护部署中的兼容性，并衡量其对记忆增长与长期检索效率的影响。

### Research synopsis

**English**

The paper addresses limitations of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) in biomedical QA caused by single-pass retrieval and lexical variability. It proposes an adaptive RAG framework that (1) reformulates queries into a propositional conjunctive normal form to capture semantic variants while preserving logical consistency, (2) classifies questions as single- or multi-hop to enable adaptive processing, and (3) for complex queries performs query decomposition, multi-step retrieval, sentence segmentation, and embedding-based re-ranking to assemble intermediate supporting information. Evaluated on BioASQ Task B, the full system yields modest improvements in ideal-answer ROUGE measures versus a Naive RAG baseline and shows particular gains on complex multi-hop questions.

**中文**

本文针对检索增强生成（RAG）在生物医学问答中受限于单次检索和术语词汇变体敏感性的问题，提出了一个自适应 RAG 框架。方法包括：将查询重写为命题合取范式以覆盖语义变体并保持逻辑一致性；用分类模块区分单跳与多跳问题以实现自适应处理；对复杂查询进行查询分解、多步检索、句子级切分和基于嵌入的重排序以构建中间证据。在 BioASQ Task B 上评测，完整框架在理想答案的 ROUGE 指标上较 Naive RAG 有温和提升，且在复杂多跳问题上效果更明显。

### Thesis relevance

**English**

Overlap: both works target multi-hop retrieval and improved evidence assembly for complex questions and use intermediate supporting information. Differences/limitations: this paper focuses on domain-specific (biomedical) textual query expansion and multi-step retrieval rather than constructing a persistent, incrementally assembled knowledge graph or graph memory; it does not study agentic RAG paradigms, persistent graph memory, or entity-resolution strategies central to the thesis. Complementarity: the paper's query classification, logical query expansion, decomposition, and re-ranking methods could be incorporated into the thesis system's retrieval and triple-extraction stages to improve evidence recall before graph insertion.

**中文**

重合点：两者都关注多跳检索与为复杂问题构建和组合支持证据，且都使用中间支持信息。差异/局限：该论文侧重于领域（生物医学）文本层面的查询扩展与多步检索，而非构建持久的、增量组装的知识图谱或图记忆；它未涉及论文核心的智能体驱动 RAG 范式、持久图记忆或实体消解策略。互补性：其查询分类、逻辑查询扩展、查询分解与重排序方法可被整合到论文系统的检索与三元组抽取阶段，以在插入图之前提升证据召回。

### Writing and related work

**English**

Position this paper as an example of effective query-level preprocessing for difficult retrievals: its propositional CNF reformulation and hop classifier are useful citations when motivating query conditioning before graph construction. Note the modest empirical gains and domain focus when comparing to general-purpose multi-hop approaches.

**中文**

把该论文作为对困难检索进行查询级预处理的有效示例引用：其命题合取范式重写和跳数分类器在论证在构建图谱之前进行查询调理时是有价值的。比较通用多跳方法时应注意其实证改进较温和且具有领域性。

### Method and evaluation

**English**

Consider adapting the hop-classification module to route queries in the thesis system between vector search, agentic planning, and graph-traversal. The CNF-style query expansion could be used to generate paraphrase variants prior to triple extraction, and sentence-level segmentation plus embedding re-ranking can improve selection of candidate passages for triple creation. Empirically evaluate these components as ablations on MultiHop-RAG, measuring bridge-entity identification, graph coherence, and end-to-end latency.

**中文**

可将该论文的跳数分类模块改造为在论文系统中把查询在向量检索、智能体规划和图遍历之间进行路由。CNF 风格的查询扩展可用于在三元组抽取前生成同义变体，句子级切分和嵌入重排序能提升用于三元组构建的候选段落选择。将这些组件作为消融项在 MultiHop-RAG 上评估，并度量桥实体识别、图一致性与端到端延迟。

### Future directions

**English**

Integrate the framework's query-expansion and hop-detection modules with a persistent graph memory so intermediate supporting snippets are converted into triples and appended to the graph with confidence scores. Evaluate the combined system under agentic RAG paradigms and test cross-domain generalization beyond biomedical data.

**中文**

将该框架的查询扩展与跳数检测模块与持久图记忆集成，使中间支持片段被转换为带置信度的三元组并追加到图中。将在智能体驱动的 RAG 范式下评估联合系统，并测试其跨领域泛化（超出生物医学数据）的能力。
